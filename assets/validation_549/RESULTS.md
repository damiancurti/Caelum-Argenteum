current stealth and rear physical/magical critical chance before existing caps,
using the shared 10 Anima/second continuous lifecycle. Production movement noise
consumes stealth. Critical damage is unchanged. Eligibility is captured at firing,
as confirmed by the author; both actors' positions are checked at impact.

## Reproduction and compatibility

Run `python assets/validation_549/prepare.py --directory <fresh-name>`, then:

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_549/run_native.ps1 -Directory <fresh-name> -Label contract-01 -Fixture hunter.pk3 -Script contract.cfg -Map MAP99
```

Other generated scripts are damage.cfg, melee.cfg, weapons.cfg, noise.cfg,
save.cfg, load.cfg, hub.cfg, preview.cfg and preview_carbine.cfg. Load uses
`-LoadGame hunter.zds`.
The compatibility helper copies saves into fresh directories. Its rollback
bridge belongs only with 5.4.8; return.cfg loads a copy of rollback.zds under the
new runtime. Keep original saves: the bridge cannot preserve metadata unknown to
the older engine script schema. This verifies one reversible baseline, not all
older versions. The neutral upgrade fixture comes from issue241-upgrade.
