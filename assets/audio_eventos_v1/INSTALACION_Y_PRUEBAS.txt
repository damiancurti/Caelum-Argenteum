DELIVERY STATUS
Six OGG files for five uses, including an alternative ship excerpt.
Excerpts were exported and technically verified. Event code was not modified,
and no tests were run inside the engine for this resource delivery.

1. LISTEN
Extract the complete ZIP and open ESCUCHAR.html. The page works offline.
The rock loop can be started and stopped with its player.
The tarot sound uses refined boundaries for the requested MAP01 phrase:
00:30.358146–00:33.667042. It lasts 3,309 s. The entire excerpt was shifted
200 ms later than revision 1.3, preserving its duration and fades.
It retains the musical mix, without separating instruments.
The other sounds retain their previous bytes.

2. COPY RESOURCES
- Copy the contents of src/ into the project's src/ directory.
- Copy assets/audio_eventos_v1/ into the project's assets/ directory to preserve
  sources, WAV masters, the catalog and edit timings. assets/ is not packaged in
  the game PK3.
- Append assets/audio_eventos_v1/integration/SNDINFO_EVENTOS_V1.txt to the current
  SNDINFO. Do not replace the entire SNDINFO or append the fragment twice.
- Rebuild the PK3 using the project's usual procedure.

Registering aliases makes them available; hearing them during play requires
connecting calls to the corresponding events in the current code.

3. AUTHOR-APPROVED ASSIGNMENTS

Lever: caelum/world/lever_activate
File: sounds/caelum/world/mechanisms/ca_lever_activate.ogg
A click lasting about 0,154 s, in mono so it can be positioned at the mechanism.
Play once only when the lever actually changes state. Do not emit sound if the
interaction is rejected, is out of reach or the mechanism is already spent.
Do not change the global door sound. In 4.36.0a,
CaelumHazardReleaseSwitch.Used plays door_open after confirming changed and
setting Spent. At that specific point, the new call is:

    // Clic del mecanismo después de confirmar su activación.
    A_StartSound("caelum/world/lever_activate", CHAN_BODY);

Carriage: caelum/travel/carriage
File: sounds/caelum/ui/travel/ca_travel_carriage.ogg
Excerpt 00:02–00:08; six seconds, with gentle fade-in/out.
Play once when carriage travel is confirmed, within the trip's local
presentation flow. Do not play at every calendar tick or while browsing options.
Canceling travel must stop the pending sound. Do not add six seconds to world
time or a gameplay wait merely to match the audio duration.

Ship: caelum/travel/ship
File: sounds/caelum/ui/travel/ca_travel_ship.ogg
Excerpt 00:03–00:09: the first three seconds were removed and six were retained.
Use for ship-travel presentation, with the same confirmation and cancellation
conditions as the carriage. If changing maps cuts the channel, coordinate playback
with the project's transition/arrival controller. Audio does not itself activate
a visual transition effect.

Ship alternative: caelum/travel/ship_first_seconds
File: sounds/caelum/ui/travel/ca_travel_ship_first_seconds.ogg
Excerpt 00:00–00:06. Included because “cut the first seconds” can also mean
extracting them as an excerpt. Choose only one variant per trip.

Tarot capture: caelum/tarot/card_capture
File: sounds/caelum/ui/tarot/ca_tarot_card_capture.ogg
Phrase around 00:30–00:33 of MAP01, refined to 30,358146–33,667042 s.
The final fade was reduced from 600 ms to 20 ms; the opening has a 2 ms fade.
Do not retain revision 1.1's long fade when replacing this file.
Emit once when the card is actually added to the character. Do not emit when
opening the album, examining an acquired card or attempting a rejected capture.
Preserve the music attenuation and restoration already provided by the reveal
controller. Do not change the dialogue harp.

Rolling rock: caelum/world/rock_roll
File: sounds/caelum/world/hazards/ca_rock_roll_loop.ogg
A 3,80 s mono loop derived from 00:38.30–00:42.40 with a 0,30 s overlap.
This is stone friction from the chosen recording; there is no added impact sound
or pitch change to simulate a different mass. The rolling rock must be the spatial
emitter. Start a single instance on a dedicated channel when rolling begins and
keep it looping while appropriate. Stop it when the rock becomes stationary,
stops rolling on a surface, is destroyed or leaves the map. Do not restart the
file every tic. Keep damage, impacts and physical simulation separate.
Registration in SNDINFO does not activate the loop: that condition belongs to
CaelumHazardRock's controller or its current successor.

4. SOURCES AND EDITING
The four external sounds are listed as CC0 on the selected pages.
The public HQ MP3 previews were used. Freesound's original WAV files require
authenticated downloads and were not included as though they had been obtained.
This package's masters are WAV files edited from those sources. Credits and
traceability are retained even though CC0 does not require attribution.
CA_MUS01 retains the project's own provenance and conditions.

All clips: Ogg Vorbis, 48 kHz. Lever and rock: mono. Travel and tarot: stereo.
Gain was adjusted to leave headroom; pitch and speed were not changed.
The catalog and EDICIONES_Y_FUENTES.json contain intervals, gain and hashes.
