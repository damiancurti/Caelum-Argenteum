Caelum Argenteum - original rain-cloud model (#251, 2026-10-14)

Created for Damian Curti's Caelum Argenteum project with Codex assistance.
Original procedural geometry and palette; no downloaded geometry, photographs,
game artwork, Doom assets or external textures are incorporated.

Editable design: assets/rain_cloud/DESIGN.json
Deterministic source: assets/generators/generate_rain_cloud.py
Runtime: models/caelum/weather/rain_cloud.obj and rain_cloud_palette.png

It does not define gameplay size, collision, flight, rain or lightning behavior.
