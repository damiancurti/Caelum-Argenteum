The body/equipment mesh, rig, animations and hair were authored for this
project using Blender 4.5.3 LTS. The texture preserves original Domingo RGB;
its alpha is opaque for mesh sampling. No Doom artwork is incorporated.

commit 3c701a8e52f09e69922e8b598d23be2d7dfc49e3:
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/3c701a8e52f09e69922e8b598d23be2d7dfc49e3/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj
Copyright holders at its September 2020 CC0 release: Data Collection AB,
Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier. Full terms: MAKEHUMAN_CC0.txt.
Modified head extraction, axes, proportions, reduced topology, original
Domingo UVs and rig weights; new pupil geometry. The original upstream source
and license remain in assets/source/art/domingo_3d_pilot/makehuman/.
Snow (Blender Studio) and Male head 3D scan (petemc) are visual references only;
their models/textures are not incorporated. Exact links/roles: DESIGN.json.
The author requested polished human 3D references rather than sprite-limited
anatomy. The current head/hair/grip candidate still requires visual review.

Editable source and provenance: assets/source/art/domingo_3d_pilot/.
Deterministic native export: assets/generators/export_domingo_pilot.py.
The .blend is authoritative editable art input, not generated runtime output.
The retained authoring transcript records construction history; it is not a
byte-for-byte Blender-file regeneration contract. MD3/PNG/state export from
the pinned source and Blender version is checked byte for byte.

Only CaelumDomingoPilotPlayer opts into the model. The ordinary player class,
original sprite resources, native movement, combat and furniture remain.
No Blender executable, development IWAD or isolated test PK3 is distributed.
