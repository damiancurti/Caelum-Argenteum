# Credits and licenses — package 05

This document accompanies Caelum Argenteum 4.30.0j. The PK3 incorporates the 14
ambience and weather sounds with a defined destination. 10 reserve resources
are kept out of the compilation, under `assets/audio_stock/pack05/`, until the author
assigns them an event. The two resources whose source requires legal review are not part
of the project or deliverables.

The files come from public HQ Freesound previews, converted and documented by the
package received. Before a final commercial publication it is advisable to replace them
with authenticated original downloads, retaining names, attributions and registration of
modifications.

  attributed to *Carnival of Souls* and the status of the copy and the public domain
  requires territorial verification.
- `77855.ogg` of mniukando (Freesound 395120): isolated because the listing mentions
  permission of a creator of another game without providing verifiable authorization or
  the scope of sublicensing.

Neither of those files enters the repository, patch or PK3.
