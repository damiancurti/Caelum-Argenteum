# Painted support implement sprites — 5.7.8 / #479 correction

Original project artwork generated with the built-in image_gen.imagegen tool,
using owned Caelum Argenteum equipment solely as style references. Four source
atlases cover Scepter, Orb, Censer and Effigy at three tiers. Exact prompts,
source images, reference/input hashes and deterministic registration are retained
under assets/art_source/support_sprites and assets/generators/pack_support_sprites.py.
Processing only cuts, masks near-invisible alpha speckles, resizes and registers
the painted sources; it does not synthesize replacement art procedurally.

The accepted Book and Statuette pixels, inventory icons, first-person poses and
character-hand variants are reused unchanged under their existing provenance.
The author rejected the earlier low-poly presentation. Its sources remain for
history/recovery, but its model bindings are inactive. Current equipment uses
painted 2D sprites in inventory, crafting, the world and first-person views.

Project/author: Damián Curti. Repository LICENSE.md applies; no third-party or
Doom artwork is incorporated. These are candidates pending author visual review.
