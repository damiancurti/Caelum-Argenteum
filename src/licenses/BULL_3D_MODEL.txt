topology; locally rebuilt forehead and horns, as approved by the author on

review requested a more symmetric head, defined eyes and a bovine muzzle.

horizontal pupils, with a broad muzzle, recessed lateral nostrils and lips.
The stocky body, tail and brown/ivory palette are retained. No additional external mesh or Doom art is
incorporated. Original project bull sprites remain available for rollback.
