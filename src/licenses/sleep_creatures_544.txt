Caelum Argenteum - living creature sleep poses, #238 / 5.4.4

Generated for this project using the built-in image_gen tool and project-owned
rat, Bull, Mandinga, Zupay and resting Argento reference artwork. No Doom art,
third-party photographs or downloaded character assets were used as sources.
Exact original/revised prompts, reference/output SHA-256 hashes and rejected
variants are retained in assets/source/art/sleep_544/BRIEF.json and revisions.json.
The Argento reference supplies pose/camera orientation only, not character identity.

The optional deterministic Pillow 11.3.0 utility
assets/generators/register_sleep_creatures.py performs silhouette cropping,
alpha cleanup, resampling and texture registration. Its calibration data live
in sleep_creatures.json; output hashes are in validation_544/sleep_art.json.
Eight views each: rat and Bull naturally lying; Mandinga and Zupay supine.
These are living poses, not death frames. Existing owned art remains intact.
