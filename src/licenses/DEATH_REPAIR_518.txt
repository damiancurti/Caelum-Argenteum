Caelum Argenteum - Domingo and giant-rat death-frame repair, issue #137.
Author/project: Damian Curti / Caelum Argenteum.
The author supplied complete original Domingo, giant-rat and bull sheets on
2026-10-09. Domingo now uses unchanged original pixels through native TEXTURES
clips. The rat uses deterministic original-sheet extraction, preserving RGB and
existing valid alpha; only missing silhouette regions need background separation.
Seven original rat death poses occupy eight existing states (last corpse held),
and nine minor movement/pain border cuts are restored. All 118 bull sprites were
audited; no clipping repair was necessary. No actor or gameplay rule changes.
Sources, extraction data and registration: assets/source/art/death_repair_518
and assets/source/art/animal_recovery_518. Earlier image_gen reconstructions,
their prompts and legacy PNGs remain unchanged for history/rollback. The old
reconstructed runtime atlases remain unused; they are not the current bindings.
Native TEXTURES changes the presentation only; actor classes, Death state
indices, duration, sounds, collision and gameplay remain unchanged.
No external or Doom artwork is incorporated into the recovered assets.
