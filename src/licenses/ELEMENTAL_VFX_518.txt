Caelum Argenteum - original elemental VFX and projectile models, issue #137.
Author/designer: Damian Curti. Integration: Codex, 2026-10-08/09.

Original transparent projectile/status/breath atlases were generated with
OpenAI image_gen for this project. Source PNGs, exact prompts, the retained
status draft and registration metadata are in assets/source/art/elemental_518.
Runtime copies preserve the source pixels unchanged. Native TEXTURES and OBJ
UV coordinates provide clipping and animation; no external raster art was used.

Arrow, bolt, bullet, pellet and javelin geometry and the cosmetic flame/ray
planes are original deterministic meshes authored for this issue. Editable
dimensions and generators are retained in assets/. Physical surfaces reuse
Caelum Argenteum's original training-dummy wood/iron atlas from issue #98.
No third-party model or Doom artwork is included in these new assets.
