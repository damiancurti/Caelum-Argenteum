CA235-02 | Origin: 5.3.8 / #235 | Current-objective map symbol
Prerequisites: isolated Probar235Objetivo terminal from
assets/validation_538/targets_handoff.ps1. This is diagnostic stage/knowledge
setup, not campaign progression. CA235-01 and all earlier checks are accepted.
1. The initial map has a target symbol at Argento's known room. F7 switches
   the current objective to Caella: Argento returns to its ordinary symbol,
   with no Caella marker while that room is unknown. F8 records Caella's room;
   her target symbol now appears. F6/F7 move it between the known destinations.
2. In Journal > Missions, T or Track/Untrack hides/restores the objective symbol
   while preserving the ordinary place and other followed missions. Check zoom,
   rotation and your normal map mode. Save to a new test slot and reload.
3. F9 completes the diagnostic mission: its target symbol disappears and the
   ordinary place remains. F10 exits; Probar235Objetivo restarts the original copy.
Expected: recognizable current-objective symbol at known recorded positions;
no unknown destination reveal, stale previous target, new route or reward.
