   Walk north to Y=2280; five guns stand at X=-1200/-600/0/600/1200.

2. Observe the open/closed breech, aimed tube, muzzle discharge, recoil and

   approved small projectile moves at full speed and may be hard to see.

   zero damage under the preserved collision formula; defenders injure their

   in hostile lanes unless deliberately checking injury in this disposable run.

   and remaining work takes twice as long; the machine is not neutralized.
   Kill the other nearby attacking operator. Expected: it neutralizes once and
   cannot fire again; any already released projectile still completes its flight.
5. Save under a new name during loading, reload, and compare status.
   Expected: positions, operators, work, ammunition, damage and neutralization
   persist. Repeating `give CaelumDebugCannonTrial` creates no duplicates.
6. `changemap MAP06`, then `changemap MAP03`. Expected: retained trial state,
   no refill or duplicate machinery. This isolated trial grants no port victory.
