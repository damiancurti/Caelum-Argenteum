CA246-01 | Origin: 5.4.13 / #246 | Mage Arcane Sun
Status: pending; defer the manual launch and questions until the author returns.
Prerequisites: Windows GZDoom 4.14.2, development IWAD, no other GZDoom instance.
Prepare: python assets/validation_5413/prepare.py --directory issue246-author
Launch: powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_5413/launch.ps1 -Directory issue246-author
1. Press F. During the three-second hands preparation, Anima decreases progressively;
   F again cancels without refund or cooldown. F6 resets the isolated scene.
2. Complete F preparation: a large fire orb travels straight through the enemy,
   burning it and emitting guided fire once per second while a visible target remains.
   The ally stays at 100000 Health. The 60-second reuse starts at release.
3. Let the orb expire: one explosion and 25 smaller outgoing projectiles, including
   vertical directions. The overlay retains final counts for inspection.
4. Press F7 and cast toward the wall: an earlier explosion, again once with 25 shots.
   The enemy behind the wall must not burn through it. F6 restores open flight.
Expected: readable scaled fire effect, no allied damage, no duplicated cast/explosion,
and the already accepted hand preparation/input lockout. Report any failed or partial step.

CA253-01 | Origin: 5.4.14 / #253 | Mansion residents at the protected 1-HP floor

Use the prepared issue253-final-v2 fixture, or prepare a fresh issue253-author directory

   the bull and let it reduce the player to 1 HP; remain alive under the existing floor.

   downed residents retain their own recovery/downing behavior. No resource refill

   demonstrate ordinary combat balance. Report any partial or failed step.

protection, finite tools and quest progression. No campaign save is loaded by this fixture.

CA260-01 | Origin: 5.4.15 / #260 (partial) | Ordinary mass-scaled acceleration
Status: pending; defer until the author returns. This does not accept the missing double-tap impulse.
Prerequisites: no other GZDoom instance; Windows GZDoom 4.14.2 and development IWAD.
Use the prepared issue260-author-ready fixture, or create a fresh directory with
python assets/validation_5415/prepare.py --directory issue260-author-new
Launch: powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_5415/launch.ps1
(For a fresh directory, add -Directory issue260-author-new.)
1. Press F6 (50 kg), hold forward from rest: the ramp reaches 100% in about 1.5 seconds.
2. Release and stop. Press F7 (100 kg), then repeat: about 3 seconds. F8 (200 kg): about 6 seconds.
3. Try running and lateral movement at each mass. The top overlay shows mass,
   progress and real speed. Release movement: normal friction stops the character;
   moving again starts a new ramp. No double-tap behavior is implemented yet.
Expected: continuous acceleration and full speed at the indicated times, with at
most one initial input tic plus duration rounding. No instantaneous teleport or
interference with normal stopping. The isolated fixture pins resource penalties
to make comparison easier; it does not load or change a campaign save.
