CA-4379-CITY-01
Origin: 4.37.9 / issue #77.
Prerequisites: normal current build and a fresh/unvisited MAP06; use save copies.
Steps:
1. Explore the 960 × 960 m city. Check representative houses, shops, factories
   and construction sites; confirm that their appearance and distribution suit
   a city expanding under construction. Visit the original harbor resources.
2. Cross north/west gates, all six south gates and the eastern dock arch. Walk
   the raised perimeter and climb/descend all four towers without cheats.
3. Play the southern siege with 6,000 Mandingas, one commander and 600 soldiers.
   Observe six rams/six hostile guns and twelve active defensive guns: eight
   on the south wall and four on the towers. The other 24 wall guns stay staffed
   in reserve. Observe a replacement physically climbing to a vacant gun post.
4. Complete both victory requirements, follow withdrawal, capture the Knight
   and use the endpoint sign near the original harbor. Save/reload before and
   after victory and verify that progress and rewards do not reset.
Expected: reachable streets/gates/defenses, appropriate city appearance, no
blocked stairs or early/duplicate rewards, and acceptable full-battle performance.

CA-4379-ROUTE-01
Origin: 4.37.9 / issue #77.
Prerequisites: current build, playable MAP02, copied campaign saves when available.
Steps:
1. Complete sewer Zupay/Ace objectives and use the existing final exit. Review
   the journey's provisions/time preview and arrive directly at MAP06.
2. Speak to extracted prisoners and claim their unchanged once-only payments.
   Save/reload and verify rescue/reward state; ordinary travel must skip MAP03.
3. For an already-visited old port, select its matching mode: --legacy-map06-north-city
   for the first 4.37.9 city, --legacy-map06-siege for 4.36.27–4.37.8, or
   --legacy-map06 for the pre-siege port. Load a copy and check retained layout,
   original army/casualties and progress. Preserve the original package/save
   for rollback; these modes do not transplant old actors into the new city.
Expected: correct direct travel and persistent rescue/rewards; compatibility
retains the exact visited port. Report subchecks not performed, including the
absence of any old port save. Agent checks do not constitute author acceptance.
