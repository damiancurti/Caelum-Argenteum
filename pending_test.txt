CA248-01 — Rally preparation, allied area and current-bar damage
Originating version: 5.4.17. Issue: #248.
Prerequisites: close other GZDoom instances. The frozen isolated fixture must
exist in build/issue248-delivery (prepare.py can create a fresh replacement).
Launch: powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_5417/launch.ps1
This uses isolated saves/configuration and does not load the campaign.
Steps and expected results:
1. Press F. The existing hands prepare for about three seconds while Anima is
   charged progressively. A second press cancels without refund; F6 resets.
2. Complete a cast. The class reuse starts at release. From the next second,
   your bar and the visible ally's bar rise by 100/1000 per second at these pinned
   base statistics. The enemy and hidden ally do not receive the recovery.
3. During the effect, F7 moves the visible ally outside/inside the area; F10 puts
   it behind the wall. Its recovery stops outside/behind the wall and resumes
   when eligible. F6 resets the scene if the ten-second effect expires.
4. During the effect, F11 sets your bar to 50%; F8 receives and F9 deals a controlled
   300-HP hit. At exactly 50%, expect 200 received and 450 caused. Pulses can change
   the bar between key presses; use the last-hit factor/HP displayed in the overlay.
   After expiry, those same hits return to 300. F6 resets Health/Anima/reuse.
Expected: readable feedback, appropriate recovery/membership and damage scaling,
with no duplicate preparation/payment or lingering benefit after expiry.
Status: pending explicit author confirmation. Automated evidence is separate.
