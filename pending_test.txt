CA251-01 | Origin: 5.4.20 | Issue: #251 | Investigator Arcane Cloud
Prerequisites: isolated build/issue251-release-5420/production.pk3 plus cloud.pk3;
assets/validation_5420/run_native.ps1 with -Directory issue251-release-5420,
-Interactive -Language es -Script manual.cfg and a fresh label. F6 resets the
scene; F7 refills Anima and clears reuse for another test. Targets are stationary
laboratory actors; the player is protected from damage. This is not a campaign save.

1. Press F (User4), wait through the three-second hand preparation and confirm
   seated flight on a dark translucent 3D cloud. F12 toggles the lateral view;
   F10 toggles ordinary third person. Check the cloud shape and rider position.
2. Use movement keys, Home to ascend and End to descend. Confirm native walls and
   ceiling stop the rider, and ordinary attack/block actions remain available.
3. Observe repeated Air-seal lightning on the enemy ahead. The ally initially
   close to the left and the farther neutral must remain unharmed. Fly directly
   over the ally and check its displayed Anima rises only beneath the cloud;
   the caster also recovers Anima once per second.
4. While the cloud is active, F8 applies War Cry. Its new lightning must continue.
   F6 clears this diagnostic scene; F7 permits another paid cast.
5. Cast again, then F9 immediately changes to the linked map. Confirm no crash,
   one cloud, seated flight and remaining duration without a fresh full timer.
6. F11 ends the cloud early, or wait for expiry: normal falling resumes. F6
   resets the scene. Report whether the gameplay and cloud/rider appearance pass.

Expected: the behavior above, no duplicate cost/effect or native crash. Concave
surface bands remain an identified mesh/material limitation for author review.
Static/native evidence is in assets/validation_5420; it does not replace this check.
