CA194-01 — Collision energy, pressure and cannon armor
Origin: 5.2.11 / issue #194
Prerequisites: rebuild the current branch with build_dev.ps1; use a fresh test
game. Keep a copy of any save/package pair used for comparison. GZDoom 4.14.2.
1. In MAP05/MAP08, compare wall impacts and falls with different health totals;
   repeat conscious versus stunned, and moving into stationary versus incoming
   moving bodies. Verify no repeated full impact while merely resting in contact.
2. In the siege test area, compare small/large rams and cannon shots on characters
   and common/reinforced/armored gates. Cannon hits must be devastating, but armor
   must absorb normally. Gate proxies must not multiply damage; rams take no
   automatic reaction HP damage. Use `toggle ca_debug_attributes`, then
   `ca_debug_next_page` to reach the physics page and inspect joules/HP.
3. In MAP08, trigger the falling/rolling rocks and stone ceiling mechanism. Check
   landing onto another body, continued pressure and stationary supported weight.
   Crushing must be energy-based, with no duplicate pulse from neighboring panels.
4. Save/load during a stable contact or pressure sequence; resume without a new
   full collision caused solely by loading. Verify controls remain responsive.
Expected: behavior matches SYSTEMS #194; raw energy is independent of Hmax,
each eligible body receives its own mitigated damage, and zero final HP loss is
possible through defenses. Report balance concerns separately; #195 bull changes
are not implemented or accepted by this check.
