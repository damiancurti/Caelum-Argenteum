CA282-01 | Origin: 5.4.22 / #282 | Quick-use carousel and native controls
Status: pending; author returned and resumed manual tests on 2026-10-15.
Prerequisites: current #282 production + isolated validation_5422 fixture;
prepare.py --directory <fresh-name>, then run_native.ps1 -Directory <name>
-Label author -Script manual.cfg -Interactive. This uses a separate config/save
directory. Do not launch automatically while the author is away.
1. In the bottom center, verify the selected icon is in the middle, with its
   localized name and quantity above, and smaller previous/next icons alongside.
2. Press F6/F7 to cycle, F8 to use. Verify one intended press consumes only the
   displayed item; a held key does not consume an unintended stream. Deplete a
   stack and verify the selection resolves without a ghost item.
3. Select the canteen, use it, and verify water decreases while the container
   remains. Check that the full inventory agrees with the carousel.
4. Open/close Inventory, Journal, automap and Escape menus; their controls should
   retain ownership and the carousel should not cover those views. Remap the
   native inventory controls, return to gameplay and verify the shown key works.
5. Check readability and separation from all six resource bars at your usual
   window size/HUD scale. Switch Spanish/English if desired.
Expected: correct layout, actual owned quantities/water, one intended use,
native menu/remapping behavior, no overlapping bars or stale selection.
