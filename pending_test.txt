CA164-01 — MAP01 gifts, optional crafting and assisted checkpoint
Origin: 5.1.18 / issue #164. Status: pending author acceptance.
Prerequisites: rebuilt 5.1.18 PK3, a new character and an untouched copy of an older save; native autosaves enabled with at least one slot.
1. Follow Argento -> Caella -> Ronnie -> Rulo. Confirm the permanent physical deck, fire/ice staff, Pickaxe with recipe and bolas. Practice Tarot before the Box; no essence power should be granted by the deck alone.
2. Choose and craft only your weapon. Skip armor, Seal, necklace and shield crafting, including after making an optional choice. Caella must accept fire, ice, Anima spent/recovered without Channel or another Seal. Review both Spanish dialogue and the Journal.
3. Enter the Bull fight while wounded and with depleted resources. Confirm all four helpers, a pre-fight native autosave and no added refill/repair. Die: there must be no scripted recovery. Load that checkpoint manually twice and win; receive the Box from Palomo once.
4. Store the deck in the Box for essence capture. Complete departure and check that the same tools remain owned. Load the older save copy: completed progress, choices, actual equipment and wear must survive without duplicate deck/Box or a repeated mandatory branch.
Expected: all steps match the current SYSTEMS/MAP01 contract. Report any failed step or qualification; agent tests do not replace this acceptance.
