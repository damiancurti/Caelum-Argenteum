CA-4379-CITY-01
Origin: 4.37.9 / issue #77.
Prerequisites: normal current build and a new/unvisited MAP06; use copies of saves.
Steps:
1. Enter Barracas al Sud. Explore the four added buildings, bed/workbench and
   prisoner area; climb and descend all four corner towers without cheats.
2. Cross north/south/west gates onto exterior ground and the east gate to both
   piers, then return. Confirm the city scale, walls and views are satisfactory.
3. Play the northern siege with its full forces. Observe rams/guns, defeat the
   commander and neutralize all twelve hostile machines, then follow withdrawal.
4. Capture the Knight, use the endpoint sign west of the northeast tower, and
   save/reload. Check that victory, rewards and army state do not reset.
Expected: all places are reachable; no stuck tower stairs, accidental level
transition, early victory or duplicate reward; acceptable full-battle performance.

CA-4379-ROUTE-01
Origin: 4.37.9 / issue #77.
Prerequisites: current build, playable MAP02, and a copy of your campaign save.
Steps:
1. Finish the sewer Zupay/Ace objectives and approach the existing final exit.
   Confirm the port journey after reviewing its provisions/time preview.
2. Arrive directly at MAP06 without visiting the MAP03 workshop. Speak to any
   extracted prisoners and collect their existing payments once; save/reload.
3. If continuing a campaign that already visited the prior siege MAP06, rebuild
   with run_dev.bat --legacy-map06-siege and load its copied save. Verify the
   old port/army/progress are retained. Preserve the original save/package for
   rollback; do not load that old port with the expanded geometry.
Expected: direct campaign travel with prerequisites and costs retained; rescued
prisoners/rewards survive arrival/reload; compatibility mode preserves old state.
Report subchecks not performed, including absence of an older port save.
