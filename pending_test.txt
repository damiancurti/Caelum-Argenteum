CA-43621-IMPACT-01 | Origin: 4.36.21 / issue #49
Prerequisites: rebuilt current PK3, GZDoom 4.14.2; copy an existing campaign
save with access to a wall, rooted tree and stationary rock. Use a character
whose current Agility/load gives a positive absorption fraction; no active
shield defense, and health high enough to survive repeated contacts.
Steps:
1. From outside combat, collide hard enough with a tree and a rock to take
   damage, including a pain response. Check health, adrenaline and combat time.
2. With unchanged equipment/load and movement condition, walk then run into
   a wall; repeat with a tree and a rock initially at rest. Check absorption
   through the existing physical diagnostics (compare fractions, not raw damage).
3. Check that a moving rock does not receive the new stationary-obstacle
   fraction; repeat idle/airborne and immobilized cases, then ordinary combat.
4. Save/reload the copied campaign and repeat a stationary scenery collision.
Expected: scenery damage/pain give no adrenaline or combat refresh. Running
and walking use the same existing fraction against walls/stationary scenery;
running can still cause more damage because speed differs. Moving rocks and
combat retain prior absorption rules, ordinary combat still gives adrenaline,
and saved progression remains intact. Author acceptance remains pending.
