CA309-01 | Origin 5.5.0 / #309 | Native weapon input audit
Prerequisite: the #309 preview terminal/game, or run the validation_550 manual
fixture described in RESULTS.md. No other GZDoom instance may be running.
1. F6 selects sword; hold R for eight seconds. It must finish charging once,
   expire normally, and not begin another charge until release and a new press.
2. Repeat with F7 (wand). Release and press R again; fire as soon as that new
   charge completes, before its three-second prepared window expires.
3. F8 selects an empty shotgun with ammunition. Hold Fire: it must reload and
   fire. Select F8 again and hold R: the reload must complete without restarting.
4. F9 selects gauntlets. Approach a visible dummy and use both mouse buttons;
   both strikes work, including the uppercut. Ordinary damage/costs remain.
5. Save during a charge or reload, load it, and confirm usable equipment/ammo.
Expected: one manual action per press, working automatic reload and no stuck
weapon. Close GZDoom and report the result. Agent checks are separate evidence.
