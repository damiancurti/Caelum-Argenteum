CA414-PROTOTYPE-01 | Origin: 5.8.0 / #414 | PENDING
Prerequisites: focused issue414 worktree, GZDoom 4.14.2, built current PK3 and
assets/validation_580/prepare.py. Close any other GZDoom session first.
Launch assets/validation_580/author_terminal.ps1 and select 1.
1. Close the console. From the overview, F1 opens the gate. The high left pool
   falls and the low right pool rises until both reach 112 units (F6 reports).
2. Restart the private room. Open with F1, then close with F2 midway: both levels
   stop; F1 resumes. The dry observation walkway remains dry.
3. F3/F4 enter either basin; verify swimming/immersion. F5 returns to dry ground.
4. Save midway using Escape, both with gate open and closed in separate saves.
   Reload: closed water stays stopped; open water continues without resetting.
   The terminal's option 2 loads a private .zds without resetting the test state.
Expected: coherent visible levels, gate collision, immersion and saved state;
no crash or unrelated flooding. This accepts the private feasibility stage only.
Quality mixing, campaign placement and calendar evolution are not implemented
or covered by this check. Existing campaign saves are not used by this fixture.

CA410-CORE-01 | Origin: 5.8.3 / #410 | PENDING
Prerequisites: issue410 worktree, prepared assets/validation_583 fixture and
current full package, GZDoom 4.14.2. Close other game instances first.
Open assets/validation_583/author_terminal.ps1 and select 1.
1. In Character (F5), inspect the contamination icon, value and bar. Hunger,
   Hydration, Sleep and thermal exposure must remain legible and separate.
2. Close the menu; F1 sets 1,000 Life, contamination 60, Toughness 0 and no
   regeneration. Expect 10 Life lost per second; F4 reports exact values.
3. F2 sets Toughness 100: damage stops without erasing contamination.
4. F3 sets full Life, contamination 0.5 and accelerated diagnostic regeneration.
   The half-degree clears in about one second, consuming Hunger/Hydration;
   Life must not exceed maximum. These rates are private test aids only.
5. F1 then F2; save/reload through Escape. Contamination remains; terminal
   option 2 loads that private save without resetting its values.
Expected: readable HUD, progressive resisted damage, full-Life depuration and
save continuity. This does not test food lots/recipes/furniture, still in progress.
