CA408-01 | 5.7.0 | issue #408 | Water quality and collection
Prerequisites: GZDoom 4.14.2, frozen #408 runtime and isolated checks prepared in
build/issue408; use the author launch left open by the agent. This is a private
test scene and does not use or overwrite campaign saves.
Steps:
1. Open Inventory (Tab). Select the canteen. Check its ES/EN water quality.
2. F1 enters potable water; F2 biological; F3 salty; F4 chemical; F5 mixed.
   R collects water into the selected container, V empties it, Enter drinks.
   Also test the Collect water / Empty buttons with the mouse.
3. Empty and collect potable water; it can be drunk when thirsty. Collect an
   unsafe source; it cannot be drunk. Topping up unsafe water with potable
   water preserves its contamination. Unsafe immersion alone does not refill.
4. Save/reload the private scene. Confirm amount/quality and inventory selection
   remain usable. Empty, then refill potable water to recover the vessel.
Expected: exact conserved liters and weight, readable labels, no unwanted
collection, purification, duplicated water or unsafe consumption. Boiling is
the separate #409 patch and is not expected in this check.
