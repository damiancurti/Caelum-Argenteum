CA255-01 | Origin: 5.3.0 / #255 | Seal/Tarot visual acceptance
Prerequisites: build 5.3.0; a character with an equipped Seal and selected Tarot.
Steps: inspect ready, unavailable, active and cooldown icons; resize the window
and compare 4:3, 16:9 and ultrawide. Try small/large native HUD scaling and both
languages. Open/close menus, save/load and return through an allowed hub route.
Expected: Tarot stays above Seal on the right edge; captions remain readable,
resources do not overlap, timers/visibility agree with the character. At uncapped
reference scale Seal is 88x88 and Tarot 64x96; crowded viewports shrink the entire
column to fit. No power activation or state change occurs from viewing the HUD.
Report any overlap and whether this layout is accepted. #256 separately adds debuffs.
