CA241-01 | Origin: 5.4.7 / #241 | Telekinesis
Prerequisites: isolated issue241-handoff runtime, Duende profile; launch with
assets/validation_547/launch.ps1. The fixture fixes force at Intelligence 25
before personality (1,700 N), gives a visible 10,000 Anima test reserve,
disables regeneration and protects player health.
1. Aim at the Mandinga and press F (User1). Move the aim sideways/upward:
   the body should follow, remain unable to attack, and consume Anima.
   Press F again to release; holding F should not toggle repeatedly.
2. F6 resets Mandinga; F7 selects a rat, F8 a Bull and F9 a heavy rock.
   The lighter body should move more readily and consume less Anima.
   Heavy bodies may not lift at 1,700 N. F10 toggles the Intelligence-100
   reference (8,000 N); the same rock should then be easier to lift.
3. While holding a body, F11 leaves a tiny Anima remainder. The power should
   stop and the target should regain its actions, retaining the remainder.
4. Recreate a target with F6-F9 and confirm it remains solid against the room
   floor/walls; ordinary gravity should resume when released in the air.
Expected: stable capture/release, mass-dependent movement/cost, suppressed
target actions and intact native physical contact. Close the game when done;
the terminal remains open. Report pass/failure for CA241-01.
