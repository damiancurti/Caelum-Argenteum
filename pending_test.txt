CA406-01 | 5.7.3 | #406 | Source recovery and saved reserve
Prerequisite: the prepared #406 private game/terminal; no campaign save required.
Aim at the hive and press E: each press extracts up to 50 g from its 500 g reserve.
F3 displays the remaining amount. F2 advances 100 game days in this private test:
expect 50 g recovered, capped at 500 g. Repeated F3 queries grant no extra 50 g.
F4 saves and F5 loads; expect the same reserve, with normal tiny elapsed recovery.
After advancing with F2, extract again with E. Press F6 (rewind 100 days), then F2:
returning to already credited time must not grant another 50 g.
F1 displays state/help without refilling. Small fractions from ordinary play are normal.
Confirm the sequence, then close the game so later isolated tests can run.
