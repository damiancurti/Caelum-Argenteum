CA259-01 | Origin: 5.4.1 / issue #259 | Weapon dispersion and switching
Prerequisites: use the isolated #259 session prepared by Codex, with the matching
5.4.1 production package and session.pk3 (not an ordinary campaign save). The
author is away; offer this check only after the author returns. Automated native
checks already cover all numeric tier/threshold boundaries; this check is for
physical input and visible behavior. F6/F7/F8 are fixture-only bindings.
Steps:
1. Fire the equipped staff at the distant training dummy or wall. F6 cycles
   Lucidity 100%, 50%, 49.9%, 10%, 9.9%; F7 cycles equipment T1/T2/T3.
2. Compare multiple shots at the same distance: lower Lucidity and higher weapon
   tiers widen the spread. Attribute precision and crouching still narrow it.
3. F8 cycles staff, book, statuette, bell, sword, carbine, shotgun, bow, crossbow.
   Try their normal primary/secondary attacks; book projectiles still seek the
   known target. The bell/shotgun T3 low-Lucidity spread stays capped.
4. Confirm that changing weapons/tiers remains usable and no weapon gets stuck.
Expected: the selected weapon/tier matches the test notice; dispersion changes
as above, without an additional Lucidity accuracy divisor. Resources, ammo and
low-Lucidity stun are held for this comparison only. No permanent test grants
or debug bindings belong to ordinary gameplay. Report the ID and any failure.

CA261-01 | Origin: 5.4.2 / issue #261 | Equipped-Seal resistance presentation
Prerequisites: the isolated #261 session prepared by Codex. Offer it only after
the author returns; no author pass is inferred from native automated checks.
Steps:
1. In Journal > Character, inspect the elemental resistance rows. The initial
   Fire Seal should improve Fire only (the Fire/Light pair); other rows keep
   their existing body-average armor value. No resistance bars are added to HUD.
2. F6 cycles Water, Earth, Air, Quintessence, no Seal, then Fire. Each matching
   pair gets 50% passive reduction; Quintessence adds 15% to every element's
   retained damage. These combine multiplicatively with the existing armor
   average, so the displayed total need not be exactly 50% or 15%.
3. Open Inventory and select a Seal. Its description must state the matching
   elements and reduction while equipped, in Spanish or English as configured.
4. Equip/unequip through the normal Inventory controls. Check that the Character
   rows update once, without accumulating bonuses from owned or boxed Seals.
Expected: readable, correct descriptions and immediate resistance updates. The
native matrix separately covers direct/area HP, fractional DOT, save/load and
hub travel. Temporary test Seals belong only to this isolated configuration.

CA254-01 | Origin: 5.4.3 / issue #254 | MAP02 rat movement and sound
Prerequisites: use the isolated #254 session with final production/session files.
Offer only after the author returns. The fixture protects the player, points the
camera toward an existing rat and does not respawn or add any enemies.
Steps:
1. Let the rat approach and bite; listen for its recognition and bite sounds.
2. F6 selects the next of four original MAP02 sections. Repeat the approach and
   listen nearby, then back away through a narrow walkway to check pursuit and
   ordinary distance attenuation.
3. F7 damages the selected rat by a small test hit; repeat if its native pain
   chance does not enter Pain. F8 kills it to check the death cue.
4. Listen with several nearby rats: cues should remain intelligible without
   excessive overlap. Check that they suit rats and have no clicks or harsh peaks.
Expected: living rats pursue and bite normally in all four sections; alert,
bite, pain and death have distinct appropriate sounds. Dead rats stay dead and
leave their existing one-time drop. F6/F7/F8 are isolated fixture bindings only.
