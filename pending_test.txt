CA387-01 | 5.6.7 | #387 | Individual clothing object sprites
Prerequisites: launch the prepared #387 test with the current package and checks
addon using assets/validation_567/run_native.ps1 -Checks -Script interactive
-Interactive and a unique -Label. The isolated CLTHQA room grants all 25 garments
in the Magic Box, known recipes, materials, workbench, sewing machine and chest;
25 additional garment copies are laid out on the floor. Spanish controls/UI load
automatically. Do not enter the game's exec command in PowerShell.
1. Close the game console, inspect the Journal's Clothing/Ropa inventory rows
   and Magic Box. Scroll through outfits, hats, gloves and footwear. Each garment
   must show its own legible icon, with clean transparent edges.
2. Withdraw several contrasting garments (formal outfit, poncho, cap, gloves,
   alpargatas), drop them and pick them up. Ground sprites must match the garment;
   no piece should turn into the formal outfit or generic cloth after a transfer.
3. At the workbench/sewing machine, select several clothing recipes and confirm
   their preview icons match the same garments. Save and reload, then inspect
   carried and dropped pieces again.
Expected: distinct, consistent art across inventory, crafting and ground items;
existing size, weight, moisture, equipment and armor behavior stay unchanged.
Scope: this checks the delivered object artwork. Worn-character, character-preview
and first-person garment coverage are separate unfinished work under #387.
