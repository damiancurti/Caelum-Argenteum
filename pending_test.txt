CA-43627-PORT-01 | Origin: 4.36.27 / issue #16 | Ordinary campaign and port battle
Prerequisites: fresh campaign or a save that has never visited MAP06; normal
4.36.27 build, GZDoom 4.14.2. Keep the original save/package.
Steps: follow MAP01/Fool -> MAP02/Ace -> MAP03 -> MAP06 without diagnostic map
commands. At the port inspect the six gate lanes, defenders and cannon platforms;
fight the siege, lower Zupay to half health and neutralize all attacking machines.
Capture the Knight with the Box, collect any owed prisoner rewards and Use the
completion sign. Try rest/repair resources before and after the battle.
Kill one operator of a staffed ram/cannon while leaving reserves alive; watch a
replacement walk to the vacancy. Spend at least 30 seconds inside the enemy
formation and check that low-Anima attackers keep pursuing/using melee.
Expected: 100 Domingo-appearance soldiers defend gates/operate guns with the
specified sword, kite shield and medium armor; automatic unlimited cannons prefer
enemy operators and take 10 seconds with two crew/20 with one. Zupay runs to the
exit; both objectives are required. Survivors withdraw physically; the distinct
Knight is captured once, rewards remain correct, and the endpoint acknowledges
the playtest ending without a diagnostic-map transition. Report visual, combat,
navigation or responsiveness problems; agent probes do not establish acceptance.

CA-43627-SAVE-01 | Origin: 4.36.27 / issue #16 | Author save continuity
Prerequisites: copies of port saves; retain originals and their original build.
Steps: on current MAP06 save/load during combat, during Zupay's flight and after
victory/card/payment. Also save/load while a replacement walks to a post or
enemies recover Anima; assignments and pursuit must continue without extra
spawns. For a campaign that already visited old MAP06, launch with
--legacy-map06 (plus --legacy-map02 when needed), load a copy,
save it separately, reload; then verify the untouched original with its old build.
Expected: no duplicated army, machine reactivation, early victory, repeated card
or reward. Flight/withdrawal continue. The legacy option retains the former map
and progress rather than placing a new siege into visited geometry. Rollback uses
the untouched original save/package.
