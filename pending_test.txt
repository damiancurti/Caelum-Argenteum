CA902-MODELS-01 | Origin: 5.18.5 / #902
Prerequisites: replacement sculpts from 5.18.11 / #916 and hardware model rendering.
Steps: view Mandinga, Zupay and the giant rat from the front, side and rear, inspect Mandinga's shirt, waistcoat and trousers and Zupay's tunic and mantle, then watch each walk, attack, react to damage and die in a test encounter.
Expected: modeled faces, distinct horns and hooves on the demons, and a recognizable rodent muzzle, paws and tail, intact action timing, no missing model or return to a billboard. Report the actor and pose for any correction.

CA903-FAMILIES-01 | Origin: 5.18.6 / #903
Prerequisites: 5.18.12 / #917 with hardware model rendering, in the inspection room or a third-person game.
Steps: inspect both sexes in all seven families, including front, profile and back. For each female form inspect the elbow and bust from the side. On humanoids check the crown, temples and nape as you cycle the four hairstyles and face shapes. Compare humanoids with the witch/warlock references and goblins with Pombero. Change skin or fur tone, humanoid face, hair style and color. Walk, crouch, attack and reload a save.
Expected: distinct family anatomy, robust male and curvier female silhouettes, proportional hands and feet, rustic facial detail and persistence of the selected appearance. Report a station and viewing angle for any correction.
