CA252-01 | Origin 5.4.21 / issue #252 | Seal climate and channel presentation
Status: pending revised author check after ground/Water image feedback.
Prerequisites: use the prepared interactive #252 scene with the matching runtime
and fixture from build/issue252-visual-05. To reopen, use a fresh label:
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_5421/run_native.ps1 -Directory issue252-visual-05 -Label author-recheck-01 -Script manual.cfg -Interactive

1. Press F to channel Fire. Look down slightly: native chasecam should show the
   matching Seal flat on the ground below you, twice the trap size. Move/jump:
   the mark follows the ground rather than rising with your feet. Water must show
   its blue spiral, not a face. F8 offers a close ground view in this fixture.
   Stop with F: the mark disappears and the previous camera mode returns.
2. Press F6 to select each next Seal (Water, Earth, Air, Quintessence), then F to
   start it. Check that each has its own image and the same camera lifecycle.
   F7 offers a wide view: an elemental orb of Arcane Sun's size must float above
   your head, follow you and disappear on stop. It is only visual.
   F6 is a fixture-only reset/selector, so no 60-second wait is needed here.
3. With Fire, Water and Air, use J and the Journal Climate page to inspect the
   local temperature, humidity and wind. Their increase grows while channeling
   and is removed on stop; humidity cannot exceed 100%. Regional weather is not
   replaced. The fixture pins magical power to x1.7 for reproducible observation.
4. With Earth, observe the growing camera tremor and outward movement of nearby
   grounded bodies. Report whether its presentation is comfortable. With
   Quintessence, observe suspension and release, then ordinary gravity afterward.
5. Confirm that stopping each Seal leaves movement/camera usable and causes no
   crash. Report any failed or unclear step; partial results remain pending.

Native formulas, damage, geometry, save/load, old-save migration and travel are
already checked separately in assets/validation_5421/RESULTS.md. This entry asks
for author gameplay and visual acceptance, not repetition of those probes.
