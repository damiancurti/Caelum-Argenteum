CA136-01 | 5.1.7 | Issue #136 | Shotgun appearance and ordinary use
Prerequisites: GZDoom 4.14.2 and the development IWAD at the documented runner
paths; no other engine instance. Prepare the isolated inspection room:
  python -X utf8 assets/validation_517/prepare.py
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_517/run_native.ps1 -Label author-136-a -Package production.pk3 -Addon visual.pk3 -Map CA136 -Script manual.cfg -Interactive -Language es
Use a fresh label for every launch; close normally. The fixture provides a
shotgun and cartridges. Fire twice, aim, reload from empty, fire once and reload
partially. Check two-handed grip, recoil, opening/loading/closing, no missing
poses and coherent independent weapon/hand alignment. Compare the smaller, muted
gloves and under-stock aimed placement with the carbine. Set ca136_mode 2 or 3 for
T2/T3; ca136_mode 5 demonstrates reloading an empty gun with only one cartridge,
and ca136_mode 6 demonstrates a partial reload. Wait for each reload to finish.
For carbine comparison, set ca136_mode 7 and wait for the equipment change;
return to the shotgun through the equipment menu.
For the world gallery: ca136_mode 1; r_drawplayersprites false; set ca136_view
0 through 47 (six poses, eight directions). Restore r_drawplayersprites true.
Check inventory/Box, independent cartridges, equipment changes and save/reload.
In normal play verify Palomo's former shortbow option, armory cartridge stock
and MAP02 cartridge pickups. Longbow/crossbow must retain their own ammunition.
Expected: coherent T1/T2/T3 shotgun, two-shot capacity, separate reusable hands,
one/two-cartridge reloads matching inventory, no ammunition interchange or crash.
Native mechanics and migration have separate evidence; this is author acceptance.

CA136-02 | 5.1.7 | Issue #136 | Thermal action timing after calor-136 report
Prerequisites: rebuilt 5.1.7 development package, original saves backed up.
Steps: on a fresh character or a safe earlier save, compare stationary shotgun
and carbine shooting/reloading, then briefly walk, swim or push against a wall
and release the controls. Watch exposure while resting and after immersion.
Repeat swimming normally/with the running command and blocked pushing with high
attributes: enhanced jumping must not amplify either effort profile.
Compare individual jumps and dagger/sword/greatsword attacks at normal and high
attributes with the same equipment. Each action has its own fixed work budget;
faster repeated attacks can still produce more total heat. Compare fast/slow
firearm reloads and interrupt one: only completed progress should add heat.
Inspect Resilience's description: at 100, adaptation is +/-10 C at 2 C/world day.
An older offset above that limit must return gradually, not jump on loading.
Save/reload while safe and repeat. Existing overheated calor-136 keeps its prior
exposure and missing HP; it is a diagnostic case, not a promised survival test.
Expected: effort adds heat only during the actual action. Stopping leaves stored
body heat to exchange with the environment, without continued activity production.
Swimming/action heat and current climate may still oppose cooling during effort.
Confirm gameplay behavior separately from the recorded native checks.
