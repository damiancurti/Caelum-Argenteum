CA-43624-JOURNAL-01 — Quest journal categories and records
Origin: 4.36.24, issue #35.
Prerequisites: current build on GZDoom 4.14.2; a new campaign and a recovery copy
of an existing save. Full port campaign placement awaits #16; do not substitute
the provisional Knight appearance for a completed siege.
Steps:
1. In a new game, open Journal > Quests before Palomo's guidance: no future
   quests should be revealed. Accept his guidance; mansion appears Main/Active.
2. Use C/right trigger and V/left trigger to inspect type/status independently,
   including empty categories. Use arrows, PgUp/PgDn, F/Y detail and Tab/B.
   Repeat in English/Spanish and 4:3/widescreen.
3. Complete the mansion and enter the sewers. Mansion remains Main/Completed;
   sewers appear Side/Active and complete on confirmed Zupay defeat or escape.
4. Release any prisoners. Each appears independently as Side/Active. Escort one
   alive to extraction before the boss: it becomes Side/Completed even before
   payment. An unrescued captive stays hidden; a follower who dies must not be
   shown as rescued/completed. Rescues must not become mandatory progression.
5. Collect a survivor's existing port reward. Its completed rescue changes only
   the payment note. Repeat dialogue, close/reopen Journal, save/load and travel:
   no duplicate reward or record. Existing recorded progress survives old saves.
Expected: clear localized title, category and actual status; completed history
stays accessible; no fabricated old progress or future-story entries. Confirm
this ID explicitly after the ordinary route passes. Full #16 siege presentation
will be checked with its own campaign deployment.
