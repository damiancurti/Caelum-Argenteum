CA242-01 | Origin: 5.4.6 / #242 | Supernatural Breathing
Prerequisites: isolated issue242-final runtime, Caelith profile and real giant
gauntlets; launch with assets/validation_546/launch.ps1. Regeneration is disabled
for visible resource changes; the player is not invulnerable.
1. Press F6 to reset. With the racial off, run (Shift + movement), jump (Space),
   attack and toggle blocking with B. Air should decrease; Anima should not pay
   these physical efforts.
2. Press F6, then F to activate the racial. Repeat: Anima should decrease and Air
   should stay unchanged. Standing idle on land with blocking off costs nothing.
   The left racial icon indicates on/off and has no cooldown timer. Pressing F
   again stops it; holding F should not repeatedly toggle it.
3. With F active, press F7 to enter water. Breathing spends Anima, not Air. F9
   empties Air: while the racial can pay, the character should not drown.
4. Press F6 to refill, F to activate, then F8 to leave a tiny Anima remainder.
   The next effort/breath turns the racial off and spends ordinary Air, retaining
   that remainder. F10 returns to land; F6 resets the scenario at any time.
Expected: consistent reserve choice, clean toggle/exhaustion, paid underwater
breathing, and no free Air refill from previously spent Anima. Close the game
when done; the terminal remains open. Report pass/failure for CA242-01.
