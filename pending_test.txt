CA380-01 | 5.6.9 | Issue #380 | Cartridge assembly and kit sprites
Prerequisites: use the isolated #380 terminal/game prepared by the agent.
The room supplies 1,000 carbine kits and 1,000 shotgun kits in the Box, a recipe
book with both sheets, learned recipes, Workbench and Ranged Workshop, plus two
ground pickups. No console item grants are required. Close the game console to play.
1. Inspect both ground kits and their inventory/Box icons and names.
2. Use the workbench, select the ammunition category and each ten-cartridge recipe.
3. At 25/50/100% efficiency check the preview requires 40/20/10 matching kits.
   Weight must show 1.600/0.800/0.400 kg carbine, 1.800/0.900/0.450 kg shotgun.
4. Complete a batch of each: exactly ten existing cartridges appear and only the
   matching kit count decreases. Close/reopen the station during one task to resume.
Expected: distinct readable sprites; correct Spanish labels; no unexpected item
grants or loss; both recipes operate through the normal crafting UI.
