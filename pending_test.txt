CA260-01 | Origin: 5.4.15 / #260 (partial) | Ordinary mass-scaled acceleration
Status: pending; the author has returned. This does not accept the missing double-tap impulse.
Prerequisites: no other GZDoom instance; Windows GZDoom 4.14.2 and development IWAD.
Use the prepared issue260-author-ready fixture, or create a fresh directory with
python assets/validation_5415/prepare.py --directory issue260-author-new
Launch: powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_5415/launch.ps1
(For a fresh directory, add -Directory issue260-author-new.)
1. Press F6 (50 kg), hold forward from rest: the ramp reaches 100% in about 1.5 seconds.
2. Release and stop. Press F7 (100 kg), then repeat: about 3 seconds. F8 (200 kg): about 6 seconds.
3. Try running and lateral movement at each mass. The top overlay shows mass,
   progress and real speed. Release movement: normal friction stops the character;
   moving again starts a new ramp. No double-tap behavior is implemented yet.
Expected: continuous acceleration and full speed at the indicated times, with at
most one initial input tic plus duration rounding. No instantaneous teleport or
interference with normal stopping. The isolated fixture pins resource penalties
to make comparison easier; it does not load or change a campaign save.
