CA902-MODELS-01 | Origin: 5.18.5 / #902
Prerequisites: replacement sculpts from 5.18.11 / #916 and hardware model rendering.
Steps: view Mandinga, Zupay and the giant rat from the front, side and rear, inspect Mandinga's shirt, waistcoat and trousers and Zupay's tunic and mantle, then watch each walk, attack, react to damage and die in a test encounter.
Expected: modeled faces, distinct horns and hooves on the demons, and a recognizable rodent muzzle, paws and tail, intact action timing, no missing model or return to a billboard. Report the actor and pose for any correction.

CA903-FAMILIES-01 | Origin: 5.18.6 / #903
Prerequisites: hardware model renderer and the character inspection room or a third-person game.
Steps: inspect both sexes in all seven families, including front, profile and back. Change skin or fur tone, humanoid face, hair style and color. Walk, crouch, attack and reload a save.
Expected: distinct family anatomy, robust male and curvier female silhouettes, proportional hands and feet, rustic facial detail and persistence of the selected appearance. Report a station and viewing angle for any correction.

CA904-EQUIPMENT-01 | Origin: 5.18.7 / #904
Prerequisites: hardware renderer and the model inspection room.
Steps: inspect the held weapon, shield and worn armor stations from all sides. In third person equip and remove a weapon, shield, helmet, torso piece, gloves and boots.
Expected: each piece occupies its own socket and follows the actor. Removing it uncovers the base clothing. Note the item, family and angle of any clipping.

CA905-BULL-01 | Origin: 5.18.8 / #905
Prerequisites: model gallery or a map containing a bull with the 3D model enabled.
Steps: inspect the forehead, horn spread, muzzle, brisket, withers and hooves from front and side. Watch the walk and charge in play.
Expected: a recognizable bull in every view with the original gait and attack timings.

CA909-PAINT-01 | Origin: 5.18.9 / #909, source #906 / PR #908
Prerequisites: gallery with the painted texture package integrated.
Steps: view Domingo, the bull, Pombero, Cuero Unudo and Nahuelito from the front and both sides under the same light.
Expected: the supplied materials follow the original UVs across body, head and face. Identify the station and angle for any further paint correction.

CA913-ROOM-01 | Origin: 5.18.10 / #913
Prerequisites: the model inspection terminal and GZDoom 4.14.2.
Steps: use 1 through 6, Page Up and Page Down, and walk around several exhibits. Close the game and use the open terminal to re-enter.
Expected: one enclosed room, a single stationary row with a readable name at each station, and working navigation and relaunch.
