CA243-01 | Origin: 5.4.10 / #243 | Battle Cry native playtest
Prerequisites: run assets/validation_5410/launch.ps1 from this branch after preparing
its isolated package. GZDoom 4.14.2; no ordinary campaign save is changed. The test
fixes a 40 m radius, 10 s duration, 1000-Anima cast, no regeneration, invulnerable
player, immortal creature and high enemy combat reserves; these are fixture settings.
1. Press F. Hands prepare for 3 seconds, Anima drains to zero, then the enemy's
   intimidation counter starts at 10 s and class reuse at 60 s. The creature may
   move but cannot attack. After expiry it can attack again.
2. F6 resets the scene. Start F and press F again before release: preparation
   stops, spent Anima is not refunded, and no effect/reuse begins. F6 resets.
3. F7 intimidates you for 10 s. Attacks and F are blocked, but movement and B
   blocking with the gauntlets still work. After expiry attacks resume.
4. F8 swaps gauntlets/staff. Repeat F7 and attack checks with the staff. F6 resets.
5. F9 cycles Mandinga, rat, Bull and Zupay. Repeat F to observe each creature's
   attack interruption and later recovery; no boss immunity. Close the game and
   report CA243-01, including any failed or partial step.
