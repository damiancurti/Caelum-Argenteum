CA133-01 | 5.1.3 | Issue #133 | City interiors and physical trade controls
Prerequisites: GZDoom 4.14.2 and the development IWAD installed at the runner's
documented paths; no other GZDoom process. From the repository run:
  python -X utf8 assets/validation_513/prepare.py
  python -X utf8 assets/validation_513/prepare_legacy.py
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_513/run_native.ps1 -Label author-133-a -Package production.pk3 -Addon manual.pk3 -Interactive -Language es
This separate inspection fixture grants a Magic Box and 100 gold and relocates
only the player. It does not alter shop stock/prices or soldier behavior. Use a
new label for another launch; close the game normally when finished.
Steps: at the harbor house, operate both door leaves, enter all three rooms,
use/release chairs and bed, and walk back out. Inspect additional city houses
and factory workstations without collision traps. In the console use ca133_visit
1 through 6 to visit each shop category. Press the bound Use key while facing
the vendor; navigate rows, change quantity/mode, buy, equip/use an item and sell
another eligible item. Reopen the shop and save/load. Repeat a menu with language
enu; restore language es if desired. Ordinary campaign entry remains supported
without the fixture's grants or relocations.
Expected: usable furnished homes and empty bathrooms; existing station rules;
Domingo-looking independent static merchants; T1 stock, correct money/quantity,
readable ES/EN rows, working physical keys, no repeated stock or quest crossover.

CA133-02 | 5.1.3 | Issue #133 | Siege behavior and shared carbine appearance
Prerequisites: the same fresh inspection session as CA133-01. Buy a matching-size
carbine/ammunition from shop 3 and equip it through the normal equipment menu.
Steps: use chase to inspect idle, walking, firing, reloading and crouching, then
switch to the existing sword. Turn around a soldier to inspect all eight views.
Enter ca133_attack true to set the authored 15 November 1889 13:00 boundary.
Observe soldiers exiting houses and moving toward posts; use ca133_visit 7 for
the southern front. Save during deployment and reload; leave/reopen MAP06 if
desired. Observe ranged fire/reload and close-range sword selection during combat.
Expected: the same surviving soldiers move physically through doors, respond to
combat and reach their posts without duplicates, teleportation or resurrection;
infantry temporarily yields access and returns. Shared art matches the held
weapon/action, with acceptable scale, direction, pivots and hand alignment;
pain/death and other weapon appearance remain intact. Player ammo is finite;
soldiers reload real magazines without physical infinite ammo stacks. This is
author visual/gameplay acceptance, not a claim of stable full-battle 35 tics/s
and 30 presented FPS. The default fixture uses #132's staged attack; a separate
fresh launch with -Staged 0 retains the full-army alternative.

CA133-03 | 5.1.3 | Issue #133 | Sweating and post-exertion cooling
Prerequisites: the same fresh session as CA133-01; a carbine and finite ammunition.
Steps: observe Thermal and Thirst before and after firing several rounds. Stop
and allow cooling; inspect equipment wetness, then compare a sheltered location
and an exposed one. Save/reload while wet and continue observing. Recover Air
without firing, running or blocking, distinguishing passive thirst/sweat from
the regeneration itself.
Expected: shots retain action heat; positive exposure can produce sweat that
costs actual Thirst and wets clothing. Evaporation continues after exertion;
wet-clothing cold remains possible. Save/load preserves accumulated state.
For its original 5.1.3 version, Air recovery itself consumes no Thirst. When
checking in 5.1.4, use #140's explicit quarter-Health recovery costs and distinguish
them from measured sweating. This outstanding check does not presume that the
existing per-shot heat is a balanced sustained-fire profile.
