CA-43719-NECKLACE-01 | Origin: 4.37.19 / issue #96
Prerequisite: new MAP01 character, or a save before choosing an amulet.
1. Follow Palomo upstairs; open his equipment menu and review the four amulets.
2. Confirm one. Check its actual T1 item in Inventory and the receipt message.
3. Reopen the conversation. Finish Caella's trial and ask for the amulet recipe.
Expected: correct chosen T1, one copy, no forced equip or changed accessory;
Caella teaches its recipe/components and never offers a different selection.
The original four equipment choices and first-weapon crafting still work.

CA-43719-SHIELDS-01 | Origin: 4.37.19 / issue #96
Prerequisite: access to Palomo's upstairs equipment dialogue.
1. Inspect all four shield options, description and confirmation pages.
2. Confirm a shield; compare the plan and its eventual crafted inventory name.
Expected: type/name agreement (ES: rodela, escudo de lagrima, escudo de torre,
escudo magico; accented in game). English uses buckler, kite shield, tower
shield and magic shield. Text remains readable with the normal UI settings.

CA-43719-SAVE-01 | Origin: 4.37.19 / issue #96
Prerequisite: keep an original pre-4.37.19 save and its original PK3 separately;
use a copy with an amulet choice, preferably an existing owned T1 or active task.
1. Load in 4.37.19. Check choice, owned item IDs/placement and tutorial progress.
2. If the same T1 is being crafted, finish or cancel that task normally.
3. Save to a new slot, reload and talk to Palomo/Caella again.
Expected: at most one new gift, existing matching T1 reused, choice/progress and
other equipment preserved. A pending full-inventory delivery reports failure
and retries after making room; repeats/reloads never duplicate a settled gift.
