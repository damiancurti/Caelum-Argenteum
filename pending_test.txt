CA-43611-GATES-01
Origin: issue #19, implemented in 4.36.15; retains the issue's planned 4.36.11 ID.
Author confirmed the other gate checks on 2026-09-26. Outstanding: corrected
player/gate damage when colliding at high speed.
Prerequisites: current rebuilt 4.36.15, GZDoom 4.14.2, disposable MAP03 save,
same character and high-speed approach that reproduced the failure. God mode off.
1. Use the existing active gate trial or enter give CaelumDebugGateTrial after
   the MAP03 gallery has appeared. The intact row is Y 2600, X -1200/0/1200.
2. Run into a closed gate at the previously tested high speed. Confirm that the
   character receives the damage determined by the existing physics/defenses,
   and that sufficient impact energy also damages the gate. Low-energy contact
   can legitimately remain below either body's canonical damage threshold.
3. Remain against the gate: touching several collision blocks must not repeat
   the same crash. Step away, then make another high-speed approach from the
   opposite side; a new separated collision must resolve again.
4. Save/reload after a collision. Previously inflicted damage must persist and
   must not be applied again merely by loading. An open/broken gate must allow
   passage without phantom collision damage.
Expected: reciprocal physical damage, correct separation/rearm and no duplicate
or open-passage impacts. Report character profile and approach if damage differs
from expectations. Earlier accepted gate/art/Use checks need not be repeated.
