CA148-01 | Origin 5.2.13 / issue #148 | Native appearance and hands
Prerequisites: rebuild the current PK3 with run_dev.bat; GZDoom 4.14.2.
Cursor follow-up: on a fresh fullscreen launch, check the pointer in MainMenu,
Options and Create Character. Report the launch/transition that hides it, if
it recurs. Clicks worked and Alt+Enter restored it in the author's 2026-10-11
report; the isolated repeat started visible. No cursor fix is claimed yet.
1. Create characters of each race/sex. Check all three tones; Beast Men also
   offer wolf, bear, puma and jaguar. Rotate/walk the preview by mouse and
   PageUp/PageDown/Backspace. Check Spanish/English and your normal resolution.
   Humans, goblins and Caelith must each show four distinct faces, four hairstyles
   (short/long straight/curly) and black/brown/blonde/redhead/gray/white hair.
2. Start a game. Compare the preview with chase camera and bare first-person
   hands. Check standing, walking, crouching, attacks, rest and death; assess
   the shared pose quality, head/body joins, skin/fur tones and proportions.
3. Equip/unequip real gloves. Check fists, shield blocking, melee/ranged weapons,
   carbine, shotgun reload and magical weapons for hand alignment and clipping.
   Broken worn gloves should stay visible; boxed gloves should not.
4. In Options > Player Setup > Appearance, change tone, beast form where offered,
   or humanoid face/hairstyle/hair color; cancel,
   then confirm. Race/sex/profession/stats must stay fixed. Save/load, restart,
   travel and return; verify appearance and sex-based pain/breathing persist.
5. Load another character (including an older save): its appearance must remain
   independent. A pre-appearance save starts at its race's default tone/form;
   an initial 5.2.13 save keeps those choices and defaults face/hair once.
Expected: supported options match the preview, world and hands; no armor/weapon
grants or gameplay changes. Confirm visual acceptance or identify the exact
race/sex/tone/form/face/hair, pose and weapon needing adjustment. Shared pose limitations
are documented in ASSETS; this is not a complete new animation catalogue.
