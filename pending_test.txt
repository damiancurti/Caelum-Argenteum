CA130-01 | 5.1.0 | Issue #130 | Thermal state and protection
Prerequisites: rebuild the current branch; use a copied save or new game.
Steps: open Journal > World > T in Spanish and English. Play through the
mansion/sewer route; compare dry clothes with immersion and drying, equip or
store a wet armor piece, and inspect the thermal state while resting.
Expected: readable air/comfort/exposure/wetness and penalties; no claim of core
temperature; wet pieces retain identity; protection and drying change exposure
gradually. Harmful cold/heat interrupts accelerated rest and gives the displayed
consequences. Confirm whether the provisional pacing feels appropriate.

CA130-02 | 5.1.0 | Issue #130 | Combat and journey integration
Prerequisites: a character with physical and fire/ice weapons, access to travel.
Steps: walk, run, jump, fight and block; compare ordinary play with a harmful
thermal state reached during the route. Preview foot and available cart/ship
journeys. Inspect the displayed refusal when a forecast is unsafe.
Expected: effort and magic change exposure; heat raises qualifying Air/Thirst
costs and cold slows movement/attacks. Unsafe departure explains cold/heat and
consumes nothing; covered transport supplies shelter but cannot guarantee every
long journey is safe. No instantaneous travel HP loss is invented.

CA130-03 | 5.1.0 | Issue #130 | Persistence and populated gameplay
Prerequisites: retain an untouched 5.0.6 save; test copies in new slots.
Steps: load the old copy, verify inventory/resources/quests, save and reload;
travel between the mansion, sewer and port and return. Play the populated siege
with the same background/audio settings used previously.
Expected: existing progress and new exposure/moisture persist without resets
or duplicate inventory. The complete army remains present. Report new stalls,
errors or conspicuous thermal behavior; earlier FPS limitations are still known.
