CA409-01 | Origin: 5.7.9 / issue #409 | Boiling and water temperature
Status: outstanding; deferred until the author returns.
Prerequisites: full #409 build, GZDoom 4.14.2 and private fixture under
build/issue409. No other GZDoom process. Open assets/validation_579/launch_author.ps1
with PowerShell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -NoExit -File; press Enter
when ready. The prepared terminal waits for that input.

1. F1 resets the private inputs: one small bottle and canteen, each with 1 L
   of biologically contaminated water, plus coal and nearby Workbench/Alchemy.
   In inventory, select water and press H. Cancel with C in the crafting page.
   Confirm no coal, volume or treatment-induced temperature/quality change.
2. Start again and finish normally or with the ordinary wait control Y.
   At 1 L / 20 C the estimate is about 461 world seconds and 66 g coal; real
   work scales with the game's world clock. Completion yields about 0.973 L
   at 100 C and removes biological contamination only. Hot drinking is blocked.
3. Wait to a later date/time after completion, or allow time to pass. Both
   bottle and canteen must cool gradually; clean water becomes drinkable at
   85 C or below. Waiting may use automatic supplies under the existing rules.
4. Without an active task, F2 cycles biological+salt, biological+chemical,
   all three flags, then biological only. Boiling must retain salt/chemicals;
   cooling must not purify them. F1 restores the initial test inputs.
5. Save/load a paused boiling task and resume; confirm one fuel charge and one
   output. Save/load hot water; its temperature must persist and keep cooling.
Expected: UI, reservation/cancellation, purity, heat and save/load work without
engine/VM errors. Confirm CA409-01 explicitly; agent tests do not imply acceptance.
