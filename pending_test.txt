CA-4368-RESCUE-01 — originating 4.36.8 / issue #14.
Prerequisites: build the normal dev package with build_dev.ps1, start a fresh
MAP01 route, reach the four keyed MAP02 prisoner cells, and later travel to the
MAP06 port.
Steps:
1. Open a prisoner cell and release that prisoner; confirm the cell dialogue
   offers release and the freed actor follows without counting as a kill.
2. Fight ordinary MAP02 enemies with a freed prisoner and confirm it uses the
   source character's combat stats while staying back from the northern Zupay.
3. Reach the pre-boss extraction reservation with the follower alive, before
   killing the northern Zupay; confirm extraction and that killing the boss is
   not required.
4. Repeat for all four prisoners, including one that dies before extraction.
5. Travel to MAP06 and claim each rescued prisoner's port reward; confirm
   +10 own-faction reputation and 25 gold coins once per rescued prisoner,
   independent of character size, with no duplicate payout on retry/save/load.
Expected results:
- Each freed prisoner follows and fights with its source combat profile and
  never participates in the northern boss fight.
- A follower that dies before extraction is not rescued and does not appear at
  the port; zero, one or four rescues are all valid.
- Each extracted prisoner persists across save/load/travel and appears once at
  the MAP06 port.
- Each port claim grants +10 reputation with that prisoner's own faction and
  25 gold coins (1,000,000 copper) exactly once; a failed coin delivery stays
  retryable without duplicate money or reputation.
- The four new faction domains appear without relabeling accepted IDs 0-3.
