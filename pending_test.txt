CA312-01 — Sustained pressure and release
Origin: 5.5.3 / issue #312.
Prerequisites: the isolated validation_553 preview and its visible terminal; no
other GZDoom instance. If reopening, run assets/validation_553/preview.ps1 with
PowerShell ExecutionPolicy Bypass and a fresh -Label. Frozen directory:
build/issue312-preview04. The player is protected; masses are diagnostic only.
Steps:
1. Press F6. Observe the native ceiling descending onto the visible body and
   the accumulated ceiling-damage counter increasing while it crushes.
2. Press F7. Two bodies appear stacked; the accumulated weight-damage counter
   increases while the upper body remains. The normal health recovery is retained,
   so use the damage counter rather than the health bar.
3. Save and load this test game while the weight is present. Pressure and its
   counters must continue, without an error or a duplicated initial strike.
4. Press F8. After the remaining partial interval is applied once, the weight
   damage counter must stop increasing. Wait several seconds to verify.
5. F10 resets the room if needed. Close GZDoom and report the result; the terminal
   remains open with these controls. Exact work/damage is already tested natively.
