CA150-01 - Readability, crosshair and comfort
Origin: 5.2.15 / issue #150. Author acceptance pending.
Prerequisites: rebuild the current branch, launch GZDoom 4.14.2 normally,
and have a character with equipment plus any second saved character.
1. Open Options > Readability and comfort. Change interface/HUD/message scales.
   Check HUD and thermal bars, inventory, crafting, creator and appearance preview
   at a comfortable small/large size. Buttons and text must remain reachable.
2. Open Journal > Notices (or L inside Journal when that key is not assigned
   to Journal/time skip). Scroll recent messages with buttons, wheel and arrows;
   long messages must remain complete, with up to 20 retained across save/load.
3. Change crosshair type, visibility, color and size. Test normal aiming, full
   automap and dialogs/menus. Fixed color requires health color off; native
   health color uses base actor life, not Caelum's derived maximum health.
4. Compare motion at rest, walking, attacking, charging, blocking and aiming
   with reduced bob. Action poses and damage/wound/Lucidity feedback remain;
   choose whether the native comfort options feel adequate in ordinary play.
5. Load the second character and restart. Global presentation choices must
   remain while each character retains its identity and notice history.
Expected: readable UI, matching clicks and acceptable comfort; unchanged
movement/aiming, attack behavior and resources. Report any layout or comfort
problem with the selected settings, language and window resolution.
