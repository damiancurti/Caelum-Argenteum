CA-503-TAROT-01 — Origin 5.0.3 / issue #119
Prerequisites: GZDoom 4.14.2; freshly built 5.0.3 or 5.0.4 PK3; a separate copy of a
5.0.2 campaign with the physical deck and captured Tarot essences. Preserve the
original save/package pair. Audio may remain at 5%.
Steps: Open Tarot in the Journal in English and Spanish. Select captured cards,
activate User3 with sufficient Anima, then change selection while effects run.
Save/reload and cross the normal MAP01 -> MAP02 route or MAP02/MAP06 hub route
with an active Fool effect. Let it expire and complete cooldown using ordinary
play/rest. Attempt capture with the deck outside and then inside the owned Box.
Open/resume Argento's Trucazo and ordinary Truco separately and use their menus.
Expected: one payment per activation; selection changes affect the next use;
correct bonuses/expiry and retained timers; flight persists/reconstructs without
extra payment. Failed captures grant nothing; successful captures occur once.
Each match keeps its own hand/progress and the existing bilingual controls.
Agent fixture checks are recorded separately in assets/validation_503/RESULTS.json.

CA-504-AUTHORITY-01 — Origin 5.0.4 / issue #120
Prerequisites: GZDoom 4.14.2 and freshly built 5.0.4 PK3; a separate copy of an
accepted campaign save with equipment, Box/deck and captured Tarot. Keep the
original package/save together for recovery. Audio may remain at 5%.
Steps: Load the copied campaign and also begin a fresh character. Check normal
movement, weapon slots, equip/store/drop/pickup, crafting or repair, and the
Journal's inventory/Tarot selections. Use the usual Tab/M/B/R/F/T bindings or
your remapped controls. Activate a captured power, save/reload, follow the normal
MAP01 -> MAP02 -> MAP06 route and return within the MAP02/MAP06 hub. Resume
Trucazo and ordinary Truco separately where their normal context permits.
Expected: each action affects the current character; exact equipment wear,
owned items, crafting reservations, resources, cards and remaining power timers
survive. No duplicate item/reward, extra activation payment or changed control.
MAP06 retains its accepted army and gameplay; no performance improvement is
promised. Report any input/selection regression or new failure to load a save.
This acceptance does not require testing unsupported multiplayer; agent native
owner-isolation and measured performance evidence are in validation_504.
