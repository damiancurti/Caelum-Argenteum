CA211-01 | Origin: 5.2.19 / issue #211 | Horizontal and fall cushioning
Prerequisites: rebuild with run_dev.bat; use a test character with and without
an active buckler. MAP01 retains its 1 HP floor; use a normal map to assess HP
loss from falls. Native reproducible diagnostics: assets/validation_5219/RESULTS.md.
Steps:
1. Walk, run and crouch into walls/stationary scenery; compare light and loaded
   characters. Low impacts within 150% of the current jump-speed capacity should
   cause no impact damage before defenses, including at Agility 0.
2. Compare falls and active/inactive buckler. The same 150% applies to biological
   landings; buckler enhances capacity once. Incoming bodies/moving scenery must
   not gain ordinary movement cushioning. Immobilization suppresses cushioning.
3. Observe the MAP01 bull charging/contact/interruption; save/load during play.
   Confirm normal charge control and that the other body's damage is not reduced
   by the bull's own cushioning. Sustained crushing should retain its prior rule.
Expected: the approved cushioning behaves consistently, with unchanged jumping,
equipment and charge timing. Report any visual/gameplay issue or acceptance.
