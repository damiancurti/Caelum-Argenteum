CA-43612-RAM-01 — Operational battering rams
Origin: 4.36.16 / issue #20 (retains planned-version test ID 4.36.12).
Prerequisites: build current source; GZDoom 4.14.2; disposable MAP03 session.
Do not overwrite a real campaign save.

1. Enter `map MAP03`, then `give CaelumDebugRamTrial`. Walk north to the six
   lanes (gates at Y=2500, X=-1500/-930/-360/210/780/1350). The last ram is large.
   Expected: five small rams with six operators each, one large with 32.
2. Observe approach, alignment, visible plate contact, withdrawal of the log
   and reuse. Run `give CaelumDebugRamStatus` between observations.
   Expected: small rams damage wood/reinforced gates; the small armored lane
   (X=-360) strikes but causes zero damage; the large armored lane causes damage.
   The plate meets the gate, the chassis does not pass through it, and each
   strike increments hits once. No new hit occurs during recovery.
3. Stand in a lane's approach path, then move clear. Expected: it waits for
   the obstruction and resumes. Avoid the striking head unless deliberately
   testing physical injury in this disposable session.
4. Kill one operator of a small ram. Expected: it stops without neutralizing.
   Kill the remaining nearby attacking operators; overlapping areas can also
   remember operators from a neighboring lane. Expected: it neutralizes once
   and stays stopped. Other living lanes continue; distant enemies do not block
   local completion. Status exposes crew presence/neutralized state.
5. Save under a new name during approach or recovery; reload. Expected:
   positions, gate damage, crew and neutralization persist; no duplicate strike.
   Repeat `give CaelumDebugRamTrial`; expect no duplicate machinery/operators.
6. `changemap MAP06`, then `changemap MAP03`. Expected: retained trial state
   and no duplicates. The isolated six-machine trial awards no port victory.

Report visual/behavioral failures or explicitly confirm this test passed.
Native automated evidence separately covers exact mid-strike/contact saves,
12-objective victory orders and obstructed/no-kill retreat. Full authored port
routes, battle rewards and cannon operation remain #16/#21.
