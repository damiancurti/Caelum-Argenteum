CA-4364-T1-LOOT-01 — MAP02 loot, recipient sizing, previews and feedback
Origin: 4.36.4 / issue #10. Status: awaiting explicit author confirmation.
Prerequisites: GZDoom 4.14.2 with the rebuilt development PK3; isolated small
and large character saves, a fresh MAP02, and a copy of a pre-4.36.4 MAP02 save.
Do not overwrite the original saves. No unrelated MAP01 replay is required.

1. With each character, Use unopened chests. Verify readable Spanish names,
   quantities, T1, appropriate essence and recipient size/properties. Cancel;
   verify no item or acquisition notice appears. Reopen and explicitly collect.
   Expected: all starter families remain available; each received item is named
   once, fits the established mapping, and has consistent weight/durability.
2. Fill carrying capacity, attempt collection, then free capacity and retry.
   Expected: only actual transfers are announced; remaining contents are exact,
   empty chests say so, and repeated Use never replenishes or duplicates loot.
3. Save/load before and after collection; leave MAP02 and return. Drop/re-pick
   representative equipment. Expected: contents, size, condition and identities
   persist. In the old-save copy, collected items (including T2/T3) remain;
   unclaimed higher-tier maze equipment is unavailable and looted holes stay empty.
4. Check representative natural ground equipment and an available equipment
   reward outside MAP02 with small/large characters. Expected: recipient sizing,
   unchanged non-MAP02 tier availability, and one accurate acquisition message.
   Crafting and merchant stock keep their prior rules.
5. Collect several items quickly and trigger ordinary gameplay notices in 16:9
   and 4:3. Expected: readable recent entries, at most 20, oldest discarded first,
   independent expiry (initially 8 seconds), no overlap with critical HUD bars.
   In shared play, another player's collection refreshes the preview and never
   duplicates loot or sends that player's acquisition notices to you.

Report any failed or partial step separately; only explicit full confirmation
removes this entry and records its result/date under 4.36.4 in HISTORY.
