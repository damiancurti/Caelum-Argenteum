CA361-01 | Origin 5.4.47 / issue #361 | NPC-only siege adrenaline (revised scope)
Prerequisites: current build and the supplied private siege preview. Older
CA318/CA327 checks are approved; no FPS evaluation is requested here.
Steps: F9 in the private preview reports NPC maxima and the player's ordinary
reserve. Channel a seal with available adrenaline and check that it spends and
can exhaust that reserve. Enter/leave a house; city return must not refill it.
Check that injured siege participants remain active while real damage applies.
Expected: only registered NPCs/monsters retain maximum adrenaline. The player's
gain, decay, penalties and seal costs stay normal in both city and houses.
Report any new discrepancy. Native correctness/save checks remain separate
from author acceptance of this newly integrated behavior.
