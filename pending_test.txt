CA-43625-MANSION-01 — Origin: 4.36.25 / issue #36
Prerequisites: rebuild current source; start a NEW game/fresh MAP01 in GZDoom
4.14.2. Do not use an old MAP01 save. The author approved the visual direction
on 2026-09-28; this is the separate final in-game acceptance.
1. Visit the highest storey under the tiled roof and inspect both end walls
   from inside and the roof/gables from outside. Expected: no unintended sky
   gaps; the east balcony passage and stair openings remain intentional.
2. Walk both levels of balconies and their corners, stairs and landings with
   representative small/medium/large characters. Expected: continuous visible
   iron guards, blocking at their actual height, no fences across walking routes.
3. Inspect exterior render/shutters, interior wallpaper/plaster, door lintels,
   stone trim and the two upper-room reliefs. Expected: materials appropriate to
   their surfaces; no floating/overlapping details, unreadable seams or obstruction.
4. Approach the outer map limit. Expected: no enormous enclosing wall or missing
   sky texture; the boundary still prevents leaving the playable area.
5. Play the mansion tutorial, speak to residents, exercise their return paths,
   complete the Bull encounter, obtain the Box and use the route to MAP02.
   Expected: established interactions, movement and combat remain usable; no
   decoration blocks doors/NPCs; rendering and performance remain acceptable.
Report passed/failed/partial with the affected view or step. Keep this entry
until the author explicitly confirms the test passed.
