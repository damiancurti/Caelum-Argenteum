Origin: 4.37.22 / issue #106.
Prerequisites: current build, a backup of an existing save, owned shield and
compatible weapon. Agent fixtures are not required or included in the PK3.

switch types/weapons, unequip, and verify broken/incompatible shields disappear.

no stale or round-shield flash, and unchanged equipment/gameplay state.
Native matrix/save evidence is recorded separately; author confirmation pending.
