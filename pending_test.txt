CA-43712-TAROT-01 — Physical deck and essence capture
Origin: 4.37.12 / issue #80. Author acceptance pending.
Prerequisites: new MAP01 run at Palomo's final Box reward, or a compatible
pre-4.37.12 save with Box/captured essences; retain original save/package.
1. Receive the Box/deck through Palomo, repeat dialogue, inspect Inventory and
   Tarot. Expect one 78-card physical deck, 780 g/one slot, and only actually
   captured essences/bonuses. An eligible old save receives its missing deck
   once; free capacity if recovery is waiting for room.
2. Move the deck outside the Box with C and attempt an available capture.
   Expect clear rejection with no reward or lost boss/quest progress. Restore
   the deck to the Box and capture normally: correct art and one new essence.
3. Try dropping/selling/breaking the deck: it stays protected. Save/load and
   complete the mansion departure; deck, essences and bonuses remain.

CA-43712-TAROT-02 — Three active powers and persistence
Origin: 4.37.12 / issue #80. Author acceptance pending.
Prerequisites: captured Fool, Ace of Cups and Knight of Wands; at least 1000
Anima. Set User3 and Fly/swim up/down in Controls if needed.
1. Journal > Tarot: Enter/A chooses up to three. Close it and hold User3.
   Expect one total 1000-Anima payment, 60-second effects and a 600-second
   cooldown from use. Try with a weapon and with fists.
   Check the remaining duration and cooldown above the side Seal indicator;
   after the effect ends, the cooldown remains visible with a gray Tarot icon.
2. Fly up/down. Character shows Ace fixed contributions +2 to each social
   attribute, Knight fixed Strength +1.2, collection +4% for these three.
   Changing the next selection must not alter the running effects.
3. Save/load while active and travel where available. Timers retain their
   remainder and advance with simulated rest/travel. No duplicate charge,
   bonus, deck or reward. At expiry flight ends and normal passives return,
   without refund. Reuse remains blocked until cooldown expires.
4. Try no selection, active cooldown and insufficient Anima. Expect clear
   rejection without a new charge/effect/cooldown. Inspect EN/ES Journal text.
