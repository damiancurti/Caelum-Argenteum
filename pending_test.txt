CA410-COOKING-01 | Origin: 5.8.19 | Issue: #410
Prerequisites: final 5.8.19 package; GZDoom 4.14.2; a disposable campaign or
the prepared #410 process fixture, with the four learned cooking recipes,
approved ingredients, potable water and coal. Author corroboration remains pending.
1. In the mansion false-wall room, inspect and use stove, oven and counter.
   Expect readable period artwork, clear approaches, no obstruction of the secret access.
2. At the counter start one bread batch (700g flour, 14g salt, 7g yeast, 420mL water).
   Expect one unattended two-hour batch; no second batch/early withdrawal.
   Wait or leave/return; the open menu updates its remaining time and readiness.
3. Collect the ready 1.141kg dough. The counter is free; quantity and contamination
   persist in inventory/save. The dough is not directly edible. Take it to the oven.
4. Start/cancel bread baking: dough and 772g coal remain, with accumulated age.
   Complete 45 world minutes: ten 100g bread portions, preserving food contamination.
5. At the stove cook/cancel the beef, fish and puchero recipes shown in the menu.
   Expect the documented quantities, 60/30/90 world minutes, ten portions per batch,
   preserved containers and cancellation resources. Automatic eating may consume a
   completed portion if Hunger requires it; this is reported separately from output.
6. Verify library access to the four learned recipes and one saved/resumed cooking
   session. No duplicate products or refilled sold recipe stock on repeated use.
Expected: all process, persistence and visual checks pass. Report any failed step.

CA411-COOKING-01 | Origin: 5.8.21 | Issue: #411
Prerequisites: final 5.8.21 package, GZDoom 4.14.2, disposable campaign;
owned deployed fire, wood, learned recipes206-208, their food ingredients,
carried potable water and a camp pot. Existing #410 checks remain separate.
1. Use the fire, select Cook (C), and inspect the three recipes in Spanish.
   Beef/fish need no pot. Puchero requires a mounted empty pot; bread is absent.
2. Cook fish using wood and no coal. Thirty attended world minutes produce ten
   100-g portions. Existing automatic eating may consume a portion afterwards.
3. Start with little wood. At exhaustion, record remaining time. Refill through
   Fire and fuel, then Continue/relight. The one-minute ignition does not cook;
   remaining food time resumes afterward, including across save/load.
4. Cancel after some burning: ingredients/water remain with their current age;
   burned wood stays spent. Extinguish and recover the unburned remainder.
5. Try puchero with no pot or a nonempty pot: reject without consuming food.
   Mount an empty pot and complete it: ten portions and exactly500mL carried
   water consumed. Beef/fish may cook beside a mounted water pot, but that water
   must not heat simultaneously. Cancelling restores its ordinary heating.
Expected: readable working controls, retained progress, exact resources and no
duplicate portions. Report the failed step, if any; do not reuse prior acceptance.
