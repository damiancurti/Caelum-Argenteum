CA158-01 | Origin: 5.1.12 / issue #158 | Pending author acceptance
Prerequisites: rebuilt 5.1.12; protected materiales-palomo save or equivalent
Palomo XS staff/shield plan with its remaining stock. Keep the original save.
1. Load the checkpoint and use the crafting workbench. Select XS, T1 and 100%
   efficiency once; the old save intentionally retains its prior M selection.
2. Preview a Seal, then return to the selected water staff and shield.
   Expected: XS stays selected; existing wood/raw sapphire and copper/tin/leather
   suffice for the remaining items at 100% efficiency.
3. Craft the selected staff and shield. Save under a new name and reload.
   Expected: both are XS and retained, quest progress recognizes them, no extra
   materials are granted and no original item disappears.
