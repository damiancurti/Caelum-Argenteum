CA131-01 | Origin: 5.1.1 / issue #131 | Thermal HUD and presentation
Prerequisites: build 5.1.1, normal game; keep a separate save slot.
Steps: inspect the new bar above Load in your usual resolution/HUD settings;
play through neutral, cold/heat and recovery, change Toughness-bearing equipment,
open/close Journal > World > T, and save/load or travel. Review the supplied
English/Spanish screenshots for all six adverse states and 4:3/16:9/ultrawide.
Expected: clear sign/marker/state; thresholds refresh with Toughness; no stale
state, clipping or overlap; resources and first-person equipment remain usable.
The bar shows exposure, while Journal keeps air/comfort/wetness separate.

CA131-02 | Origin: 5.1.1 / issue #131 | Attributes, adaptation and long travel
Prerequisites: separate 5.1.0 save backup or fresh character, 5.1.1 build.
Steps: review Constitution/Resilience descriptions, compare passive Sleep loss
when each effective attribute changes, and observe climate adaptation in the
Journal. Compare safe/unsafe long-journey previews and successful arrival;
check saved exposure, wetness, equipment and quest progress after reload.
Review native evidence for accelerated five-day limits, gradual return after
Resilience decreases and the isolated route between distinct regional climates
(current shipped destinations all share Buenos Aires).
Expected: Constitution reduces Sleep like Hunger/Thirst; Resilience 100 gives
3 C/world day and +/-15 C from the racial base, five days to either distant
limit. Existing acclimatization returns gradually after a lower cap. Long
travel accounts for route climate and passenger shelter, rejects harmful
forecasts without spending provisions, and invents no real-time thermal damage.
