CA147-01 | 5.2.0 | #147 | Creator, saved identity and native controls
Prerequisites: rebuilt 5.2.0 package, two differently configured characters and copies of older saves/packages. Preserve originals.
Steps: create characters with different race/profession/sex/height. Go back, close the creator and reopen it before confirming; inspect all choices and points. Confirm through the introduction. In Spanish and English, review profession names/descriptions and native player setup. Change global preferences, alternate saves, restart and travel between hub maps. Check equipment/wear, resources, quests and Tarot. Browse native Actions/Weapons/Inventory/Other controls and remap an ordinary action; verify the chosen mapping survives restart. Use diagnostic commands from the console if needed.
Expected: the session draft survives reopening without changing the loaded character; confirmation consumes it once. Each save retains its own identity and progress. Native setup does not offer competing race/profession/gender/skin/color selection. Abilities, flight, Journal and time skip appear in the corresponding native groups, with no Caelum debug section; console commands remain available. No new appearance assets or multiplayer support are implied. Report any failed step or qualification.
