CA407-01 — 5.7.5 / issue #407 — Marine imports, iodine and 3D materials
Prerequisites: GZDoom 4.14.2; the prepared #407 scene launched by
assets/validation_575/manual.ps1, using build/issue407/current and checks.pk3.
The terminal explains the controls in Spanish; no terminal commands are needed.
1. Press E at the Workbench connected to Alchemy. The fixture supplies the
   learned iodine recipe and 1 kg of dried cachiyuyo. At 100%, make the nominal
   batch: 100 g input, 1 g output. Wait or use F5's diagnostic work advance.
2. Confirm one consumption/output; cancel another task and confirm no loss.
   A nominal 25/50% batch must ask for a larger batch or efficiency without loss.
3. F1 opens Provisions. Buy dried cachiyuyo; check money and stock. F2 returns
   to Alchemy. Q closes the panels. Inspect both dropped objects from several
   sides with WASD/mouse: dried blades and iodine container must remain 3D.
4. F3 saves and F4 loads. Materials, money, shop stock and any active task must
   persist without duplication. F2 must still open the iodine recipe afterward.
Expected: ordinary commerce, one-time production, intact save state and readable
3D objects. Close the game when finished and report this test's result.
