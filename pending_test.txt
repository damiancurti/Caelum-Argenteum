CA377-01 | 5.6.1 | Issue #377 | Merchant transactions and session continuity
Prerequisites: use the isolated #377 package/addon and disposable character.
Keep original campaign saves separate. From the repository root:
python assets/validation_561/prepare.py
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_561/run_native.ps1 -Label author377-01 -Checks -Interactive -Script interactive
Use a new label for each launch. The frozen package is
build/issue377/current/caelum_argenteum_dev.pk3.
Steps:
1. Close the console. Inspect the nearby ranged merchant's partially depleted
   ammunition stock and the bought worn bow in your inventory.
2. Buy and sell one stack; change quantity. Confirm the displayed lot price,
   exact money change and remaining stock. Try holding Enter and releasing it;
   each accepted purchase must correspond to the displayed stock/payment.
3. Sell the worn bow, reopen the vendor, and buy that same copy back. Its size
   and durability must remain unchanged; other copies must remain distinct.
4. Close/reopen, move out of reach, and save/reload with trade open. Reopening
   must retain stock and money without repeating payment or replenishing stock.
5. Open a workbench while trading, then reopen the vendor. Crafting and trade
   views must remain mutually exclusive; a paused craft must retain its work.
Expected: familiar merchant controls and margins, exact item/quantity transfer,
clear rejections and no duplicated payment, objects or vendor stock.

original campaign saves with their matching package. From the repository root:
python assets/validation_562/prepare.py
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_562/run_native.ps1 -Label author381-01 -Checks -Interactive -Script interactive
Use a fresh label. Frozen package: build/issue381/current/caelum_argenteum_dev.pk3.
Steps:
1. Close the console. Open inventory, select the small carried chest and use it.
   Confirm its worn bow and 23 wood units, slot limit and exact total mass.
2. Withdraw a partial wood quantity, switch to Deposit and return it. Change
   selection/quantity with keyboard, buttons and mouse wheel; cancel and reopen.
   No selection or cancellation should move anything.

   reserved objects, another chest and protected Tarot/temporary items cannot
   be deposited. Verify a rejected request leaves both inventories unchanged.
4. Press C to place the chest in clear space; reopen, collect/deposit and carry
   again. A blocked placement must keep it carried with its contents intact.
5. Save/reload with storage open and travel/return using disposable saves.
   Contents, slots, exact load and native lock state must persist.
6. In a disposable campaign inspect a tutorial/labyrinth chest: existing Collect
   remains available; D opens stored objects without refilling its rewards.
   Hold/release Use and Enter; each accepted transfer must match displayed data.
Expected: readable bilingual controls, a live unpaused menu, exact transfers and
full chest mass, no duplicated or lost contents. Native scripted destruction,
capacity boundaries, migration and TID travel are recorded separately.

saves separate. Run Start-CA378Test in the prepared terminal, or from the root:
python assets/validation_563/prepare.py
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_563/run_native.ps1 -Label author378-01 -Checks -Interactive -Script interactive
Use a fresh label. Frozen package: build/issue378/current/caelum_argenteum_dev.pk3.
Steps:
1. Close the console. The character faces the new bookshelf in the room opposite
   the training dummy. Approach it and Use; alternatively open the Recipe book
   from inventory. The fixture supplies Longbow T1/T2/T3 and component sheets.
2. Select a tier, inspect the category, unread state, book mass and time. Start
   reading; close and reopen. Partial reading pauses and does not teach.
3. Complete a first reading. T enables existing x105 advance; Y offers the date
   skip with its existing survival checks. For a quick isolated UI check only,
   enter `netevent ca378_finish_reading` in the console after starting a read.
   The fixture shortcut finishes that task without changing production timings.
4. Confirm the selected tier and components become learned, other final tiers
   remain unread, sheets remain, and reread count is still 0. Complete a reread:
   count becomes 1/10, and the next reading and efficiency penalties decrease.
5. Test keyboard/buttons/mouse/category selection, cancel, out-of-range pause,
   save/reload and reuse. Check legibility in English and Spanish and the new
   bookshelf's placement/appearance. Do not overwrite campaign saves.
Expected: retained pages, separate tier learning, no materials from reading,
correct pause/cancel and gradual capped progress. Actual equipment/consumable
and recursive practice, capacities, migration and travel have separate native
scripted evidence; library sales are delivered separately under #383.

with their matching package. Run Start-CA384Test in the prepared terminal.
Frozen package: build/issue384/current/caelum_argenteum_dev.pk3.
The terminal starts the actual MAP02 test save with existing assigned drops;
no new loot tables or synthetic DropItem carriers are added to that save.
Steps:

   ammunition/key/potion drops; ordinary acquisition notices and inventory
   changes must match the objects actually received.
2. Defeat another living Mandinga or rat. Assigned loot retains priority;

   collected objects stay collected, and uncollected drops remain recoverable.
   Use disposable saves only. Existing chest-specific UI checks remain CA381-01.
Expected: unchanged current loot rules, successful acquisitions only, no repeated
entitlements. Reentrant callbacks, old/current ice phases, native resurrection
and save migration have separate scripted evidence, not a manual acceptance claim.
