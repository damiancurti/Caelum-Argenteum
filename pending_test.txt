CA-43618-WALK-01 — Walking wall absorption
Origin: 4.36.18 /issue #43.
Prerequisites: current normal build, GZDoom 4.14.2, disposable MAP03 or a
copied save with a clear wall. No active buckler/giant-gauntlet defense;
Agility above zero, normal health/air and low enough load that JumpZ exceeds 8.

1. Enable the diagnostic panel with ca_debug_attributes true; use
   ca_debug_next_page until the physics readout shows RawDV, TraumaDV and Bio.
2. Walk into a wall with directional input and effective running off.
   Expected: a new wall contact has Bio >0 and TraumaDV <RawDV. Damage may
   remain zero because Toughness still applies after absorption.
3. Step away to rearm the contact and repeat while running, then crouched.
   Expected: running has Bio=0; crouched contact retains its prior absorption.
   Check both Always Run settings using the speed key to select effective walking.
4. Jump from a flat floor and land at the same height with unchanged load and
   normal state. Expected: an ordinary self-jump causes no health loss.

Native evidence separately covers the exact 80 kg /20 kg /all-20 profile and
excludes walking benefits for actors/projectiles, idle and airborne states.
Report failures or explicitly confirm this check passed.

CA-43619-BALANCE-01 — Final siege impact balance
Origin: 4.36.19 /issue #21.
Prerequisites: normal current build, GZDoom 4.14.2, fresh disposable MAP03.
Use separate fresh sessions for ram and cannon trials; preserve real saves.

1. Give CaelumDebugCannonTrial and observe a complete discharge. Give
   CaelumDebugCannonStatus after contact. Expected: attacking lanes report
   damage 766/1194/800 for normal/reinforced/armored gates; one contact per shot.
   Two/one operators retain 30/60-second cycles. Defenders damage enemy figurants.
2. In another fresh MAP03, give CaelumDebugRamTrial. Expected: small rams
   break normal/reinforced/armored gates in 2/3/12 hits, large rams in 2/3/7,
   with complete staffing. Bodies obstructing a strike can consume it.
3. Save and reload a partially damaged gate scenario. Expected: remaining
   damage and opened/broken state persist without duplicate components/refill.
   On a copied older save, the gate preserves the fraction of resistance remaining.

The author approved these numerical values and requested merge/closure;
this entry tracks later manual observation, not another balance approval.
