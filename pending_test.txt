CA382-01 | Origin: 5.6.5 / issue #382 | Padlocks, theft witnesses and household chests
Author follow-up 2026-10-21: remaining observed defect was chest movement on a
padlock hit. Retest only step 3: the flail must break the closed padlock without
moving the chest, preserving its contents and theft response. Other steps below
remain the original reference; they do not need repeating for this correction.
Prerequisites: frozen #382 package and supplied interactive scene; no other GZDoom session.
Launch from repository PowerShell:
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_565/run_native.ps1 -Label author-382-review -Checks -Interactive -Script interactive
Use a new Label when repeating. Close the game console with the console key before moving.
1. Face the three small chests: center is foreign (ten gold coins), right is yours,
   left is public. E opens at close range. Open your chest; L removes/reinstalls its
   padlock. No theft warning or hostile witness should follow own/public access.
2. Open the foreign locked chest using the supplied picks/master key. Expect one
   theft warning and the visible nearby NPC to attack. Merely viewing the menu or
   repeating its refresh must not produce another alert. Player is invulnerable.
3. Close/reopen the foreign chest, or hit its closed padlock once with the supplied
   flail (left mouse, close enough and aim down). A blunt break opens it, preserves
   its gold and triggers the same theft rule. Picks/master keys remain reusable.
4. Behind the start are crafting stations; 50 kg steel is in the Box and recipes
   are known for this fixture. Optionally craft another lock/key pair or either tool.
5. In the GAME console (not PowerShell), enter: changemap CAH000
   Find the small locked household chest next to the table. It starts with ten
   gold coins. Remove one, leave with changemap CAH001, return with changemap CAH000;
   the remaining nine persist. Existing unrelated chests should retain their access.
Expected: correct ownership warning, visible witnesses within 32m respond, native
   contents/tools persist, and household gold does not refill on return.
Pending explicit author confirmation; automated/native evidence is validation_565.
