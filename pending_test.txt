CA-43713A-CRAFT-01 - Visible time shortcuts
Origin: 4.37.13a / issue #93. Author acceptance pending.
Prerequisites: a reachable working crafting station and a valid recipe/task.
1. Open crafting in ES and EN. Expect the bottom help to show keyboard Y time
   skip beside T acceleration, with all text readable inside the panel.
2. Begin a task. Expect its progress line to show both shortcuts. Press Y:
   the existing destination selector opens. R updates the estimate, Q returns;
   T retains acceleration. Existing recipe and controller inputs still work.

CA-43714-DECK-01 - Palomo gives the deck upstairs
Origin: 4.37.14 / issue #97. Author acceptance pending.
Prerequisites: a MAP01 save before following Palomo; retain its original copy
and package. Leave room for 780 grams in carried inventory.
1. Speak downstairs, then follow his route. Expect no deck before arrival.
2. Speak to him on the top floor. Before choosing a reply, expect one 78-card
   physical deck and EN/ES explanation, without the Box or any captured essence.
3. Close immediately, reopen, save/reload and later accept the Magic Box.
   Expect the same single deck and ordinary quest progress. Put it in the Box
   before an essence capture. With full capacity, free weight and speak again.
4. Repeat with an older save that already owns the deck: no additional copy,
   loss of deck or essence changes. Original save/package remain the rollback.
