CA-MAP02-PIT-RETURN-01
Origin: 4.37.7 / issue #74.
Status: pending explicit author acceptance; agent native checks are separate.
Prerequisites: rebuild the current source and start a fresh campaign/MAP02.
Previous-map saves were explicitly waived. Use the ordinary campaign route or
the engine's direct `map MAP02` test profile. Consult
assets/validation_4377/LOWER_NETWORK.png for positions and cardinal orientation.

1. Enter the initially open south block and step on its floor trap. Confirm a
   real fall into shallow water, no stairs/ladder, and a walkable route to center.
   Other pits are in west and east; open those blocks with their normal keys and
   repeat. Pit IDs/upper room centers: 43905 (-768,-4512), 43916 (-3744,1536),
   43925 (5280,768), 43929 (5280,-1536), 43930 (2976,1536), 43933 (2208,1536).
2. Approach each central grate from the tunnel side. Use opens the reached grate
   without a key and calls the lift down; the other three need not be opened.
   Enter, stand near an edge, face it and Use to return to the central start.
3. Repeat trips and call from both landings. Also descend from the start with
   all grates closed, then Use from inside: you must be able to return upward
   while the grates remain closed. They must not open from inside.
4. Check a still-closed surface block remains inaccessible from below, including
   by jumping beneath its trap. Its cover stays shut until the normal entrance
   opens. Cells, extraction and boss progression retain their existing keys/rules.
5. Bring released prisoners through falls and the return route; let distant
   followers catch up using their established behavior. Confirm none is stranded.
   Kill a key carrier near/in a pit and recover its key at its original dry upper
   position. No ammunition/rations/equipment should be preplaced as floor loot.
6. Save/load in a pit, tunnel, near an opened grate and during elevator movement.
   Return through the hub and repeat. Opened mechanisms and progress must persist,
   with a usable route and no duplicate supplies. Check the appearance, water,
   clearance and movement with your intended character size.

Expected: every accessible trap has a complete flooded return; exactly four
independent grates; working repeatable elevator; no locked-progression bypass,
unavoidable companion softlock, save-state reset or new water damage rule.
