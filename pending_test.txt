CA-4365-MAZE-01 — Four-section sewer, gates, refuges and compatibility
Origin: 4.36.5 / issue #11. Status: awaiting explicit author confirmation.
Prerequisites: GZDoom 4.14.2, normal rebuilt PK3 and a new MAP02 or a save that
has never visited MAP02. Keep original saves; use copies for compatibility checks.

1. Traverse all four sections with normal controls. Obtain each section and cell
   key before its lock, test a wrong key, then open with the correct one. Check
   cell beds and the route back to the extraction reservation before the boss.
   Expected: no bypass/deadlock, usable beds, broad bends/crossings/open gates;
   transparent closed bars stop passage, Use and attacks. The extraction marker
   does not transport the player or grant a rescue/reward.
2. Inspect channels, iron conduits, barred gates and refuges. Check the retained
   T1 recipient-sized loot/39 chests, food/water and accessible ammunition.
   Expected ground totals: 120 food, 120 water, 240 arrows, 120 bolts, 120 bullets.
   Population remains 96 Mandingas/one Zupay and 45 traps; no new rats.
3. Wear an owned weapon with a known recipe. Obtain required materials through
   existing finite salvage, then use a connected refuge station to repair it.
   Expected: normal material cost/time, only reserved units consumed. A weapon
   with unknown recipe cannot be repaired even if components are available;
   it may still wear out and break. No free repair/material/recipe allowance.
4. Save/load after collecting keys, opening cells and taking chest loot; leave
   and return to MAP02. Expected: gate/bed/chest/owned-item progress persists.
   Check Zupay movement/attacks in the northern arena and retained boss/Ace exit
   requirements. Live four-prisoner escort AI remains a separate #14 test.
5. For a copy of an older campaign that already visited the three-section MAP02,
   launch run_dev.bat --legacy-map02. Load inside MAP02 and from another hub map,
   then return. Expected: original layout, position, keys, loot and wear retained.
   Keep compatibility mode for that campaign; rebuild normally for the new maze.
   This preserves the old map rather than transferring it into the new layout.

Report failed/partial steps separately. Only explicit author pass confirmation
removes this entry and records the ID, origin, date and qualifications in HISTORY.
