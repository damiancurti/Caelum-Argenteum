CA184-01 | Origin: 5.2.6 / issue #184 | Author acceptance pending
Prerequisites: build 5.2.6 in GZDoom 4.14.2; a fresh or current test character with a Tarot deck and workshop access/materials.
Steps: use mouse and keyboard alternately in Truco and Trucazo (cards, calls/responses, rules, save/load, forfeit/cancel); in crafting (modes, filters, entry/detail scroll, tier/size/batch/efficiency, craft/repair/disassemble/cancel/back); and in time skip (fields, values, estimate, start/interrupt/back). Repeat at your normal resolution/UI settings in Spanish or English. Drag off a button, open/close and switch panels, then verify movement/aim/use/attack recover.
Expected: aligned pointer/highlights, one intended action per click, unavailable actions cannot transact, no accidental opening click or world input, card games pause and crafting/time skip keep the world active. Existing rules and assets are retained. Report any failure or qualification.

CA189-01 | Origin: 5.2.7 / issue #189 | Author acceptance pending
Prerequisites: build 5.2.7 in GZDoom 4.14.2; a test character with Journal, workshop, rest furniture, chest and merchant access; your normal resolution and input device.
Steps: use clicks, wheel and keyboard/controller across all Journal pages, calendar entries, inventory/equipment, merchant, chest, crafting, time skip, travel and the creator. Speak to several NPCs, including wrapped replies, More and Repeat/history. During rest, drag with the right mouse button, release and move the cursor freely, then use left-click controls. Open Escape/settings and return; save/load an open live screen; switch away from the game while dragging and return. Resume normal aim/movement/use afterward.
Expected: original images and content remain visible, aligned controls perform one intended action, disabled/obsolete targets cannot act, history grants no repeated rewards, the camera orbits only during right-drag, and input ownership recovers. Live panels/NPC dialogue continue simulation; cards and Escape/settings pause. Report any failure or qualification; real controller and physical right-drag acceptance remain author checks.
