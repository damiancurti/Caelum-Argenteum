CA162-01 | Origin: 5.1.15 / issue #162 | Author acceptance pending
Prerequisites: current branch/package, GZDoom 4.14.2 and the local development
IWAD. This isolated tool check does not test Caella's future gift (#164).
From the repository in PowerShell, prepare the local fixture:
  python assets/validation_5115/prepare.py
Then run (choose a new Label if re-running):
  .\assets\validation_5115\run_native.ps1 -Label author162-01 -Fixture inputs -Script preview.cfg -Interactive -Language es
Steps:
1. Confirm the corrected right-leaning bronze Baston, hands and equip pose. Use family key 1 to switch
   between the Pico and Baston. Inspect its inventory icon/name and Anima/s.
2. Hold primary toward the passive target for fire; release, then hold secondary
   for ice. Inspect the tip, continuous effects, burn/freeze and HUD resources.
3. Turn toward a nearby wall to inspect flame illumination and occlusion. Release
   and switch tools; no stream or source light should remain. Save/reload once.
Expected: readable bronze tool and hands, distinct fire/ice, normal concurrent
Anima regeneration, no double emission or stuck light, one persistent tool.
Existing tiered magic weapons are named Varitas. Report visual/handling acceptance
or specific defects. Close the engine normally when finished.

CA163-01 | Origin: 5.1.16 / issue #163 | Author acceptance pending
Prerequisites: current branch/package, GZDoom 4.14.2 and the local development
IWAD. This isolated test does not include Rulo's future permanent grant (#164).
Prepare from the repository in PowerShell:
  python assets/validation_5116/prepare.py
Launch with a fresh Label if repeating:
  .\assets\validation_5116\run_native.ps1 -Label author163-01 -Fixture inputs -Script preview.cfg -Interactive -Language es
Steps:
1. Inspect family-1 Boleadora and its inventory icon. In the corrected ready
   pose all three weights hang below the grip. Hold secondary: spin is 3 Hz,
   with a stable hand. Try less than one second, then longer; primary throws.
2. Miss once and retrieve the floor item; equip it through Inventory if needed.
   Aim a prepared throw at the passive
   bull. Walk around it: front cords remain visible while its body hides the
   rear arcs. Inspect visible weights, an unobstructed mouth and release after
   five seconds. This fixture fixes its Strength to zero for reliable recovery.
3. Save while preparing or while ropes are visible; reload and confirm coherent
   ownership, remaining restraint and recovery. Confirm the release/recovery
   hand animation and the size of the original artwork in motion. No bolas pose
   should expose a cropped wrist/forearm, including on a wide display.
4. For player cutting, enter ca163_stage 8 in the console. The fixture supplies
   an unequipped dagger. Try movement/attack, then press Reload to cut and escape.
5. Close the first engine, then launch the paid-escape preview:
   .\assets\validation_5116\run_native.ps1 -Label author163-fire-01 -Fixture handling -Script handling-preview.cfg -Interactive -Language es
   Prepare and throw at the Mandinga: the piece burns immediately, with a clearly
   visible brief flame. This preserves the confirmed instant escape rule.
Expected: readable three-ball tool and body-fitting ropes, no stuck input,
permanent stun, duplicate item or invisible recovery. Reload cuts without an
ordinary reload/attack. Report visual/handling acceptance or specific defects.
Close the engine normally when finished.
