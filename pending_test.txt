CA307-01 | Origin: 5.4.27 / issue #307 | Normal prisoner following speed
Prerequisites: rebuild the normal development package; use a MAP02 campaign with
one or more freed prisoners, or release one through its usual cell conversation.
1. Walk away from a freed prisoner, then stop. Expect normal walking speed without
   a catch-up boost; it should stop near you rather than pressing into your body.
2. Move away again. Expect following to resume at the same normal speed. A wounded
   or slowed prisoner should retain its ordinary movement penalty.
3. Leave it far behind, beyond the existing 1024-unit threshold. Expect the
   existing recovery teleport to nearby free space, without a speed burst.
4. Save and load with the prisoner following. Expect the same prisoner, rescue
   state and inventory, with normal-speed following and no duplicate companion.
Confirm movement feels appropriate; report any stall, excess speed or load failure.
