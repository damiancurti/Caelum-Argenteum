CA165-01 | Origin: 5.1.14 / issue #165 | Pending author acceptance
Prerequisites: rebuilt 5.1.14; original loco-map02 save protected.
1. Load loco-map02. Look around and move normally at the sewer entrance.
   Expected: the solid red overlay is gone; the scene, weapons and HUD are
   visible. Position, equipment, Health and Tarot cooldown remain meaningful;
   ordinary resource regeneration continues.
2. Save under a new name, reload, and check visibility again.
   Expected: no solid red overlay returns and the character remains intact.
3. On the next ordinary hit, observe the damage tint.
   Expected: a brief proportional tint fades, rather than staying opaque red.
