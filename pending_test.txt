CA318-01 | Origin 5.4.28 / issue #318 | Contact-only demon-breath heat
Prerequisites: rebuild 5.4.28 and use a fresh campaign. Use a reachable
Mandinga or Zupay and the normal thermal display. MAP06 performance acceptance is
separate and remains unresolved; this check does not require a full-army siege.
Steps: receive direct breath, then leave its cone/range. Observe burning heat;
repeat behind a solid wall and near breath without direct contact. Save/reload
while burning. Previous-save acceptance was waived by the author on 2026-10-17.
Expected: direct contact burns and contributes positive heat while its fire lasts
(ambient exchange still affects the net temperature);
nearby untouched bodies and the emitter receive no breath radiation. Walls block
contact. Reload preserves the remaining effect without a heat spike or reset;
Report any visual, resource,
temperature, interruption or save regression. Author confirmation is pending.

CA318-03 | Origin 5.4.28 / issue #318 | Native MAP06 doors
Prerequisites: fresh MAP06 using the normal 5.4.28 build; this check does not
require loading an old campaign or starting the full-army siege.
Current scope: front house doors now change maps (CA318-04). This check applies
to the retained native moving pairs inside the private house maps.
Steps: use interior doors from both sides, including while touching
their visible faces. Reuse an open/closing pair; stand or place a body in its
swing path. Move out of the path, walk through and check passage under the
private interior's ceiling; the old open-roof flight step no longer applies here.
Save during opening or an obstructed pause, reload, then leave/return through
the hub. Observe the retained models and sound. Compare the first street and
ground-facing FPS with the documented benchmark on the same settings.
Expected: paired normal opening, renewed wait, unobstructed passage when open,
no pushing/crushing, no rising or detached models, and finite vertical collision.
No duplicated/missing leaves after reload/hub return. Native checks and measured
gains do not establish 60 FPS or full MAP06 playability. Author acceptance pending.

CA318-04 | Origin 5.4.28 / issue #318 | Separate house interiors and shop signs
Prerequisites: fresh MAP06 in the current house-hub build. This author test
covers peaceful exploration; full-army siege performance remains unresolved.
Steps: walk around several houses and compare their size/street widths. Use a
front door, interact with the soldiers, injure one, save/reload inside, leave
and return, then save/reload outside and return again. Test internal doors and
furniture. Read the shop signs and compare each category with its vendor stock.
Expected: original outer size/roof height/street widths; correct private house
and original doorstep on return; individual resident health, finite equipment
and inventory persist without copies or replacement. Signs are legible and fit
the nineteenth-century setting. Report FPS and location/view while walking.
The brief preview was reviewed positively, with an explicit size-preservation
requirement; that is not confirmation of this complete acceptance test.

CA318-05 | Origin 5.4.28 / issue #318 | Variable-height sky diagnostic
Prerequisites: close any existing GZDoom. Use the prepared local test package;
from the repository run:
python assets/validation_issue318/house_preview.py --directory issue318-ceilings-01 --variant zoned-1000
This opens an isolated preview with its own config/saves and visible FPS.
Steps: walk several residential streets, look forward/down/up, use house and
shop doors, and inspect the city from the wall and tower routes. Try upward
movement near the low/high sky boundary and a house roof. Compare house size,
street widths and the skyline. Report any visual gaps or blocked public routes.
Expected: original house footprints and 184-MU roof height; residential physical
sky at that height; the outer wall/tower region keeps 1,024 MU. The sky remains
visible. Low districts intentionally limit flight and closed roofs cannot be
stood on. Decide whether those restrictions and the visible transitions are
acceptable before adopting this diagnostic geometry. This is not a full-army
performance acceptance test; normal production geometry is unchanged.
