CA-43711-CAPTAIN-01 — Guard recognition, amnesia and narrative review
Origin: 4.37.11 / issue #79. Status: pending author acceptance.
Prerequisites: current build; MAP06 with a living local guard not actively fighting.
Steps:
1. Approach and Use a guard. Read the first greeting and select the amnesiac response.
2. Close and speak again, or speak to another guard. Review English/Spanish wording.
3. Open TAB > Quests, main/completed filter, and read "They call me captain".
4. Review a new campaign's opening Journal and the current captain biography in MAP01.
Expected: the fortified city's guard recognizes its captain; responses and Journal
retain missing memories. First/repeat text agrees. No class/faction/equipment/
reputation/reward or guard-command changes, invented biography or premature
Limbo/Selene disclosure. Prisoner intelligence still follows actual payment.

CA-43711-CAPTAIN-02 — Saved knowledge and independent progression
Origin: 4.37.11 / issue #79. Status: pending author acceptance.
Prerequisites: copies of a pre-4.37.11 save and current campaign; use matching legacy
map options for visited old geometry. Keep originals with their original package.
Steps:
1. Load the earlier save: no captain clue should appear solely from past port visits.
2. Hear a guard's first greeting and close without answering; verify the clue exists.
3. Save with the guard conversation open, reload, close and reopen it.
4. Leave/return through a hub route and talk again after the siege is completed.
5. Check recognition with zero, some or all prisoners rescued in available campaigns.
Expected: the current open page resumes; later conversations acknowledge learned
information. No memories return, rewards/events duplicate or unrelated progress
changes. Rescues and siege outcomes remain independent. Original save/package
still provide rollback; these tests do not claim full-city performance is fixed.
