CA-43611-GATES-01
Origin: issue #19, implemented in 4.36.15; retains the issue's planned 4.36.11 ID.
Prerequisites: current normal build, GZDoom 4.14.2, disposable MAP03 session
with a created character and an existing melee/ranged weapon. Preserve real saves.
1. In the console: map MAP03. After the gallery appears, enter
   give CaelumDebugGateTrial. Go to the intact row at Y 2600:
   wood X -1200, reinforced X 0, armored X 1200.
2. Try walking through each closed gate from both sides. Use opens both leaves;
   standing in the opening must keep it open. Leave the opening and wait for it
   to close. The damaged/open gallery rows farther north remain only previews.
3. Attack an active gate with ordinary melee/projectile attacks. The first
   health loss shows its damaged state. Save, reload, and verify that state.
   These large gates have 65,017 / 170,625 / 659,083 HP; use existing character
   equipment and stats, not a forced-destruction command, to assess durability.
4. Continue until destruction. Walk through from both sides; further attacks
   must not restore collision or create duplicate effects. Save and reload.
5. In this disposable session, enter changemap MAP06, then changemap MAP03.
   The broken gate stays broken and partially damaged gates retain damage.
   Repeat give CaelumDebugGateTrial: it must not duplicate or reset active gates.
Expected: correct material states, usable intact doors, persistent damage,
clear destroyed passages, no invisible wall or closure trapping a character.
Report separately if any step cannot be completed. Group/key/faction guards
and impact-API math have native agent evidence; complete ram/cannon operation
and the full port encounter are outside this check.
