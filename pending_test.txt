CA283-02 — Guard, queued jump and landing
Origin: 5.4.23 / issue #283. Status: pending author confirmation.
Prerequisites: isolated issue283-shield-grip-01 manual trial, Domingo with sword/shield;
front review camera and separate settings. CA283-01 remains provisionally accepted.
1. Press B to toggle shield blocking. Walk with W and run with Shift+W; use F8
   to review front/side/rear. Expected: handle stays in the closed hand; shield
   faces forward while guarding, and lies beside the body in its forward/back
   plane when relaxed. The elbow bends and the legs keep moving.
2. Disable blocking with B. Tap and release Space. Expected: 10 tics of knee
   flexion/body lowering before takeoff; releasing Space does not cancel it.
   On contact with the floor, the landing flexion returns upright over one second.
3. Repeat while moving and while blocking; also try attacking during preparation.
   Expected: ordinary movement/attack/block rules still work, with no extra jump.
4. With blocking off, press F10: it queues a jump and injects damage before the
   10 tics elapse. Expected: cancellation with no later takeoff. F9 still injects
   damage directly. Wait for the ordinary hit reaction before repeating; health
   must be above 500 for test damage. This automation is only in the fixture.
Scope: this reviews the added guard/jump presentation and controls, not the still
pending interchangeable parts or timed rest/provision animations. Report by ID.
