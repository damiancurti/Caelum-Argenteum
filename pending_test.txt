[CA313-01] Ammunition mass, reload and Box
Origin: 5.5.4 / issue #313.
Prerequisites: open the prepared #313 game and terminal, using
assets/validation_554/preview.ps1. Use only one GZDoom instance.
Steps:
1. F6 selects the carbine; fire at the visible target. The top counter must
   lose one cartridge and 0.040 kg. Press R: reloading must not subtract again.
2. F7 selects the shotgun. One shot must consume one cartridge and 0.045 kg,
   while retaining the normal shotgun attack. R must not subtract again.
3. F8 transfers the selected weapon's ammunition between personal inventory
   and the Box. Check its location in inventory; total count/mass stays intact.
4. Save and reload; counts, locations and partial magazines must survive.
F10 resets the fixture. Close GZDoom and report the result when finished.
Expected: consistent 40/45-g complete-cartridge accounting, without duplicate
reload charges or lost ammunition. These are approved design approximations.
Native evidence is recorded separately in assets/validation_554/RESULTS.md.
