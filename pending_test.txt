CA244-01 | Origin: 5.4.11 / #244 | Survival Instinct native playtest
Prerequisites: run assets/validation_5411/launch.ps1 with its prepared isolated
runtime. GZDoom 4.14.2; no campaign save changes. Fixture uses 100 precision,
fixed base critical/evasion, 10000 Anima, no regeneration and an immortal target.
Health starts at 50% so the existing health-based Adrenaline factor is nonzero.
1. Before casting, press F7 and note the Adrenaline gain; inspect the overlay's
   precision, weapon critical chance and evasion. F launches the actual 3-second
   preparation with progressive 1000-Anima cost and 60-second reuse at release.
2. While active, factor is x3 for 10 seconds. Precision is 300 for both categories;
   weapon critical chance and evasion triple subject to normal caps/penalties.
   F7 grants three times the former combat gain, not nine times. F8 changes
   staff/carbine so both actual weapon categories can be checked and fired.
3. At expiry the extra factor disappears and the values/gain return to their
   ordinary amounts. F6 resets the scenario, resources and reuse.
4. After resetting, start F and press F again during preparation. No effect or
   reuse begins; spent Anima stays spent. F6 resets.
5. Optional F9 applies a real controlled incoming hit: the overlay reports the
   actual evasion chance and result. Individual outcomes remain probabilistic.
Close the game and report CA244-01, including any failed or partial step.
