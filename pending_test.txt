CA258-01 | Origin: 5.3.3 / #258 | Status: pending author acceptance
Prerequisites: issue258 build in GZDoom 4.14.2; isolated Probar258 terminal
fixture, or an initialized living character. Keep original saves unchanged.
1. Reach 0% Sleep without a bed: expect immediate visible lying in place and
   factor 1, with gradual recovery and no free refill.
2. Below/at 10%, try movement, Use, attacks, jump/crouch, Q/Back, Journal and
   time-skip: none may stand the character. After >10%, Q allows standing;
   crossing the boundary alone does not force waking.
3. Check falling, water, unsafe/moving ground, damage and critical food/water:
   lying continues with ordinary physics/hazards and real automatic portions.
4. Check real bed/bag support versus a chair or nearby furniture; save/load
   while locked, pause/resume and inspect camera/controls after recovery.
Expected: no relocation, duplicate portions, early exit, immunity, fake bed
bonus or MAP02 ground acceleration permission. Report any partial/failing step.
