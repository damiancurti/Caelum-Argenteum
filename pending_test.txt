CA239-01 | Origin 5.4.9 | Issue #239 | Hunter's Instinct
Prerequisite: launch assets/validation_549/launch.ps1 in the isolated scenario.
The scene fixes base critical chance at 20%, roll at 50%, high accuracy and slow
gravity-free projectiles, with a stationary Mandinga and 10,000 Anima. These are test
settings, not production balance. Read the displayed state/impact counters.
1. Press F: racial activates, Anima drains, stealth changes from 20% to 60%.
   Press F again: it stops and stealth returns to 20%.
2. With the target's back facing you, activate F and fire. Switch F off while the
   projectile is flying. Its eventual hit must still say critical YES.
3. Fire with F off, then activate F during flight. The hit must say critical NO.
4. Fire while active, then F6 to turn the target toward you before impact: NO
   critical. Reset with F9; with active launch and rear impact: YES critical.
5. F7 switches staff/carbine. Repeat a rear/front comparison with both weapons.
6. F8 leaves almost no Anima: racial stops and stealth returns to 20%. F9 resets
   the scene and resources. Closing the game leaves the launch terminal open.
Expected: launch eligibility stays fixed, rear geometry is checked at impact,
toggle/exhaustion cleanly restore stealth and the common racial HUD state.
