CA386-01 | Origin: 5.6.6 / #386 | Ordinary clothing
Prerequisites: GZDoom 4.14.2, current build and private issue386 checks addon.
PowerShell from the repository (close other GZDoom sessions first):
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File assets/validation_566/run_native.ps1 -Label author-live-manual -Script interactive -Checks -Interactive
Use a fresh Label if that label already exists. The prepared session contains
all 25 garments in the Box, all recipes, 50 kg of each of the five inputs,
a recipe book, a workbench, a sewing machine, sample body armor and a small
chest; no console grants are needed.
1. Close the console, open the Journal inventory and choose Clothing.
2. Retrieve a garment from the Box, then equip/unequip it. Switch body outfits,
   headwear, gloves and shoes; worn armor must remain equipped independently.
3. Check the garment name, correct size, 100/100 condition and real carried mass.
   The initial summer set adds 1.760 kg at M; only one garment per region is worn.
4. At the workbench, craft a garment. Check its actual material grades, selected
   size and output. A garment cannot be repaired or dismantled.
5. Store/retrieve a garment in the Box; save and reload. Identity, equipment and
   wet weight must survive without extra starter garments or lost armor.
Expected: ordinary clothing works independently under armor; no duplicated
   warmth/weight/items. Existing cloth icon is temporary; models are issue #387.
Report the result for CA386-01. This check is not accepted yet.
