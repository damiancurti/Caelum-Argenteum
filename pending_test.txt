CA250-01 — 5.4.19 / issue #250 — Redemption
Prerequisites: isolated GZDoom 4.14.2 scene prepared from assets/validation_5419;
launch.ps1 opens it with 5% audio and no campaign save. F6 resets the scene.
1. Press F: observe the three-second preparation and progressive Anima payment.
   At release, the center enemy corpse and left allied corpse rise with full
   resources. The living ally on the right remains alive. The overlay shows x4
   Toughness for you/living ally and x1 for bodies raised by this cast.
2. Walk away: the former enemy follows. At expiry it dies again, while the original
   ally stays alive and your Toughness returns to x1. No additional loot appears.
3. F6 resets. Cast with F, then F8 introduces another corpse: it rises on the next
   one-second pulse. Press F8 again to kill that body; it must not rise again during
   the same cast. F7 clears cooldown/refills Anima for a fresh cast.
4. F6 resets. Cast, then press F9 while active: temporary former enemies accompany
   you, retaining the remaining duration. F10 advances a one-hour journey; the
   temporary allies die and the aura ends. Original allies stay on their own map.
Expected: all four steps match; no stuck controls, duplicate bodies or repeated
death rewards. Automated save/load, defense ordering and compatibility results
are separate evidence in assets/validation_5419/RESULTS.md.
