# Pending author tests

## CA-4369-TAROT-ART-01

- **Originating version/issue:** 4.36.9 / #15.
- **Prerequisites:** build the current PK3 with `build_dev.ps1`; start a normal
  campaign on GZDoom 4.14.2 and open the Journal Tarot page.
- **Steps:**
  1. With no captured cards, confirm the collection count is 0/78 and the page
     shows the shared card back.
  2. Capture El Loco in MAP01; confirm the Journal Tarot page shows the Fool
     front and its accepted name.
  3. Reach MAP02, defeat the Zupay and capture the Ace of Cups (card 36);
     confirm the page shows the Ace of Cups front and its accepted name, never
     a card back.
  4. With both cards owned, use left/right on the Tarot page to switch between
     the Fool and Ace of Cups fronts, and verify neither owned card is hidden.
  5. Set `ca_journal_tarot_preview` to another card ID (for example 11) and
     inspect the imported front without granting the card or campaign progress;
     restore the CVar to `-1` when finished.
  6. Save, reload and reopen the Journal Tarot page; verify ownership and the
     owned fronts are preserved, with no duplicated card or reward.
- **Expected result:** approved fronts render at the accepted 4:3 and
  widescreen proportions; owned cards show their front, unobtained cards remain
  unowned, and no new power or reward is granted.
