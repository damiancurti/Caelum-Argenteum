CA238-02 | Origin 5.4.4 | Issue #238 | Living creature Sleep poses
Prerequisites: isolated MAP99 scenario in build/issue238-sleep-handoff, GZDoom
4.14.2, production.pk3 + poses.pk3. Run assets/validation_544/launch_sleep.ps1
in a terminal; keep that terminal open.
Controls apply only to this test profile. Player is invulnerable; F8 supplies a
diagnostic HP-losing impact on the selected creature, regardless of aim.
1. F7 cycles rat, Bull, Mandinga and Zupay. F6 restores resources/reuse and wakes
   the bodies. Press F (User4), wait for the three-second preparation, then walk
   around the selected body. Rat/Bull should lie naturally; Mandinga/Zupay should
   lie on their backs, with complete bodies, grounded and without opaque borders.
2. While asleep, press F8 three times within the effect duration. The first two
   HP-losing impacts keep the selected enemy lying; the third wakes it. All three
   diagnostic hits receive x3. Its ordinary pain reaction may follow waking.
3. F6, then F again. Without F8, the creature wakes after the ten-second effect.
4. Repeat for the four bodies; report clipping, wrong directions or stuck poses.
Expected: all four retain living silhouettes and wake correctly. This check does
not accept other racial/class effects or close #238. Exit the game when finished;
the terminal remains open.
