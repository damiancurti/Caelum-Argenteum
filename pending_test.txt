separate options for automated checks, each mate, MAP03 and the workshop.
1. Options 2-4: inspect each mate, its bronze bombilla and inventory/quick-use
   sprites. Prepare (R) must reopen the selected mate from Inventory. Text and
   controls must remain legible without overlapping the weight or contents.
2. R loads 20/50/100 g yerba. Select the warm canteen with arrows and F to pour
   60/150/300 mL. The source remains and loses only transferred water. Enter
   drinks; water refills do not renew hot uses. Check cold hydration below 60 C,
   hot benefits at 60-85 C and blocked drinking above 85 C.
3. At full Thirst, hot fresh yerba still restores Health/Anima/Air over ten
   seconds. Repeated doses retain earlier pending recovery. Washed yerba gives
   hydration only; empty water with V, then press C twice to discard and R to
   reload. Holding C must not discard. Optional private console command:
   netevent ca472_sample 75 1 sets one remaining use at 75 C; 59/86 test bounds.
4. Save/load with a partially filled mate and remaining uses; check that contents,
   temperature and pending recovery persist. Box/chest/drop/sell/buyback must
   retain contents, and boxed mates must be unavailable for drinking.
5. Option 6: craft the three mates with approved materials and stations. Cancel
   must spend nothing; completed crafting pays once. Option 5: buy mates/dry
   gourd in Provisions and recipes in the library; verify three/ten initial stock.
Expected: all behavior above, no crash or missing sprites. This confirms #472
only; #471 CA471-BATCH-01 is already accepted. Agent checks are separate evidence.
