CA-501-PLAYER-01 — Player adapter acceptance
Origin: 5.0.1 / issue #117.
Prerequisites: rebuild 5.0.1 for GZDoom 4.14.2; retain an original 5.0.0
package/save pair separately and use the map layout matching that save.
Steps:
1. Create a character through the ordinary menu; confirm the chosen profile,
   attributes and starting equipment. Move, run, jump, use the Journal and
   switch equipped weapons with the usual controls.
2. Spend and recover Air/Anima, take an ordinary hit and observe resource
   penalties/recovery. Confirm the accepted behavior and timing feel unchanged.
3. Load a copy of the existing 5.0.0 character, save to a new slot, reload and
   travel through the available MAP02/MAP06 route and back. Check profile,
   resources, item identities/condition and campaign progress before/after.
Expected: no profile reset, free refill/repair, lost/duplicated items or progress,
unexpected input change, error or newly introduced stutter. Confirm the result
and identify any steps not exercised; agent fixtures are recorded separately.
Known observation to assess: MAP06 benchmark throughput was 4.9–6.0% lower;
the full-scene cause remains unresolved in the automated evidence.
